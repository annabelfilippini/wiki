# BB Session: Wayloft Landing Page Design Direction

## Key Takeaways
- Landing page is platform introduction, not tool funnel — sells the dream
- "Your points, properly spent." locked as headline — understated, premium
- Steel credit card photo as hero visual, not AI-generated card art
- Playfair Display serif tried for editorial/old-money feel, then reverted — typography unresolved
- Landing page may intentionally diverge from Signal design system aesthetic

## Notable Claims
- CardArtPlaceholder gradient cards look AI-generated — real imagery needed for premium feel
- Worth-It tool moves to nav, not hero position
- Landing page and app can have different typographic identities

## New Information
- First concrete design direction for landing page (previously defaulted to Worth-It hero)
- Aesthetic references established: Stitch (premium light), steel card renders, old-money energy
- Open question: how landing page aesthetic bridges to Signal design system

## Conflicts
- Typography: Playfair Display chosen in session but reverted in code (commit 484f804). Font direction remains unresolved. Conflicts with Signal design system's Geist-only rule, though landing page was explicitly scoped as an exception.
