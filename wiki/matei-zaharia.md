# Matei Zaharia

Co-founder and CTO of [[databricks]]. Creator of [[apache-spark]] (as a UC Berkeley PhD project in 2009 under [[ion-stoica]]). Associate professor at UC Berkeley. 2026 recipient of the ACM Prize in Computing.

## Key Facts
- **Role:** Co-founder + CTO at Databricks; associate professor at UC Berkeley.
- **Origin:** 2009 UC Berkeley PhD. Built Spark to speed up big-data workloads; released as open source.
- **Career arc:** Became a tech celebrity at 28 when Spark took off. Spark became Databricks. Databricks became a $134B company.
- **2026 ACM Prize:** $250K cash award, which he is donating to an as-yet-undetermined charity.
- **Dual track:** Unusual for a Silicon Valley CTO — still actively teaching and advising students.

## Views on AI (as of April 2026)

**On AGI:** "AGI is here already. It's just not in a form that we appreciate." His frame: stop applying human standards. An AI passing the bar exam doesn't equal human general knowledge, because AIs can ingest facts trivially. Treating the models as "little humans" leads to both overclaiming and underclaiming.

**On agents ([[open-claw]]):** Simultaneously excited and alarmed. Calls the design — an AI that mimics a trusted human assistant with access to your browser and passwords — "a security nightmare." Example risk: compromised agent spending unauthorized money from a logged-in bank session.

**Most excited about:** AI for research and engineering search. Not app-building. His analogy: vibe coding made programming accessible; "accurate, no-hallucinations" research AI will someday make *understanding information* universal. Examples he cites: diagnosing car rattles, scanning radio/microwave data beyond text/images, molecular simulation for biology.
