# Personal Finance Strategy

Advice from a financial meeting. Core framework for early-career personal finance.

## Account Structure
- **Chase** -- credit cards (pairs with [[transfer-partners]] and [[wayloft]] travel rewards knowledge)
- **Fidelity** -- investments (good mobile app, retail brokerage account)
- **Credit Karma** -- credit monitoring (soft pull, no credit impact)

## Key Rules
1. **Do not use Chase for investments** -- use a third-party custodian (Fidelity or Schwab)
2. **Keep 6 months of cash in reserve** -- add up monthly expenses, keep 6x in savings/checking
3. **Never be forced to sell investments for cash** -- the reserve prevents this
4. **Hold investments for long periods** -- time in market beats timing the market

## Investment Strategy
- Put all investment money into **stocks/equities via index funds**
- Index funds track broad markets like the S&P 500 (500 largest US companies by market cap)
- **Fidelity total market index** or **Vanguard VOO** (S&P 500 ETF)
- Use a **taxable brokerage account** for non-retirement investing
