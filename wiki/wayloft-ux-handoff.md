# Wayloft UX Handoff

## Key Takeaways
- Product positioning: "Think 'Mint for credit card points'"
- Target user: 22-35, has 2-5 travel credit cards, wants to maximize value without becoming a spreadsheet person
- Tone: "Premium but approachable. Not a finance app — a travel app that happens to involve credit cards. Think Apple Wallet meets Google Flights."
- Card art placeholder colors by issuer with network badge and accent stripe
- 5 transfer currencies with airline + hotel partners, ratios, transfer times, alliance info
- Quiz flow: 5 steps (spending → credit score → existing cards → preferences → review)
- Transfer Bonuses urgency colors: Red <48hrs, amber <7 days, green >7 days
- Flight search: PriceToggle (Cash / Points / CPP), portfolio-aware card recs per flight
