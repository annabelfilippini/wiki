# Puerto Rico

Group trip itinerary with detailed budget breakdown. WWOOF farm stay + coast exploration.

## Itinerary
1. **Day 1:** Fly into San Juan, rent car, spend night in San Juan
2. **Days 2-7:** Drive to WWOOF farm (~2.5 hrs from San Juan), stay at Buena Fruta Farm
3. **Day 7:** Drive toward San Juan, stop at **Maricao Forest** for a hike, camp at **Gozalandia Falls** ($60 total for campsite)
4. **Day 8:** Explore Gozalandia Falls, drive to **Arecibo Coast**, Airbnb overnight
5. **Day 9:** Drive to airport, fly home

## Budget (per person, 4 travelers)
| Item | Per Person |
|------|-----------|
| Flights | $420 |
| Car rental | ~$80 |
| Farm housing | ~$69 |
| San Juan Airbnb | ~$52 |
| Arecibo Airbnb | ~$52 |
| Campsite | $15 |
| **Total** | **~$670** |

## Key Spots
- **Buena Fruta Farm** -- WWOOF host, camping (free) or eco-housing ($45/night for 2)
- **Gozalandia Falls** -- hike + camping
- **Maricao Forest** -- hiking stop on the drive back
- **Arecibo Coast** -- coastal overnight

## Logistics
- San Juan to Mayagüez (farm area): 2.5 hr drive or $118 one-way flight
- Renting a car is the better option ($90/person vs $118 flight one-way)
