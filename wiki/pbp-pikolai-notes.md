# Pikolai Notes

## Key Takeaways
- [[pikolai-starostin]] is a fictional AI CEO persona for Pickleball Portal
- Named after Nikolai Starostin, who co-founded Spartak Moscow and was arrested by Beria in 1942 for "praising bourgeois sport"
- Hardware: Dell Precision 5860 with RTX 6000 Ada 48GB GPU — "his compensation package"
- $500/month autonomous budget. Monthly Spending Memo to board (Tom + Annabel). 48-hour veto. All spending public.
- Voice: "Formal, dry, slightly pompous, deeply serious about pickleball"
- Sign-off: "From the top of the leaderboard, Pikolai"
- Priority Tier 1 (30 days): Fix 404s, make Pikolai operational, launch public presence
- Priority Tier 2 (90 days): Decision log, beginner path, cross-tour tournament aggregator, $500/month budget series + first merch
- Priority Tier 3 (6-12 months): Premium membership, community layer, multi-agent org under Pikolai, expansion to Padel
- Long game: "AI-native sports authority media" — pickleball is flagship, governance model is IP
