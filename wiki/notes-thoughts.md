# Notes — Autonomous System Vision

## Key Takeaways
- One unified space for all knowledge and thoughts — Obsidian as the brain dump layer
- Agents should act on knowledge: send emails, manage calendar, challenge ideas, build businesses
- BB agents should be backed by YC/gstack knowledge for business development
- Automated scanning of news, X, Reddit, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram for market niches
- Agents that ship products and distribute them to relevant communities
- System should iterate on itself and learn from work patterns + online signals
- "Open Claw" mentioned as the execution/action layer where projects get built
- Core value: **freedom** — freedom to do what she wants when she wants

## Architecture Sketched
1. **Obsidian/wiki** — brain dump, notes, personal life, ideas, everything
2. **Open Claw** — execution layer where projects (Wayloft, PBP, new ideas) get built
3. **BB agents** — business development, idea challenge, strategic advice
4. **/last30days** — market scanning across platforms
5. **Ship agent** — distribution to Reddit threads, communities that need the tools

## New Information
First explicit articulation of the full autonomous business system vision — from knowledge capture through market scanning to automated distribution. Previously these were separate tools; this note connects them as layers of a single system.

## Conflicts
None — this is a vision document, not factual claims.
