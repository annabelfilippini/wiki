# San Francisco Neighborhoods

Scouting notes for the move to SF for [[wayloft]]'s parent company Okta. In-person neighborhood assessments.

## Top Picks

### Pacific Heights (Favorite)
- Very clean, quiet, and well-maintained
- Park in the middle, more trees than other neighborhoods
- Less chaotic than North Beach or Russian Hill
- Lots of people in their 20s
- ~2.5 miles from Okta office
- Nice views
- More expensive
- Close to Marina district
- Check out: **Roses Cafe**

### Marina (Tied Favorite)
- At the bottom of the hills (flat -- good for running)
- Quiet, clean, cute houses
- Close to bars and nightlife
- Slightly fewer young people than Pacific Heights
- Further from Okta office

### Russian Hill
- Calmer and quieter than North Beach
- Small shops but no big town center
- Beautiful views
- VERY steep hills (concern for daily running)
- Further from work

### North Beach
- Diverse restaurants, close to Okta
- Massive hill to deal with
- Feels very "city" -- landlocked, surrounded by buildings
- Good going-out location
- Close to Chinatown
- Cute houses

## Decision Factors
- Proximity to Okta office
- Hill grade (impacts daily running)
- Noise level / neighborhood vibe
- Age of residents
- Access to bars and restaurants
