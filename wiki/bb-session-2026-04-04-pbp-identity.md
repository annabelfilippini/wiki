# BB Session: PBP Identity & Redesign Approach

## Key Takeaways
- PBP is a data-powered media company — content drives distribution, data drives monetization
- Consumer data product (price alerts, premium comparisons) is the lead product
- Ship, Learn, Redesign phased approach chosen over big-bang rebrand
- Each phase de-risks the next — solo founder can't afford a failed all-in bet

## Notable Claims
- Dropping content velocity would decay traffic in 6 months — SEO is not self-sustaining without new content
- Tournament marketplace requires B2B sales motion — Phase 2 at earliest
- Pikolai is brand, not product — don't confuse the persona with the value prop

## New Information
- First clear articulation of PBP's dual-engine model (content + data)
- Lead product identified as consumer data, resolving prior ambiguity
- Three-phase redesign plan with concrete scope per phase

## Conflicts
- Previous wiki content described PBP primarily as a media/content site. Now explicitly positioned as data-powered media — data is the differentiator, not just a feature.
