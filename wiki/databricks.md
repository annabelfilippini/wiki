# Databricks

Cloud data + AI platform company. Founded on the commercialization of [[apache-spark]], now positioned as "a data foundation for AI and agents." Co-founded by [[matei-zaharia]] (CTO) and [[ion-stoica]].

## Key Facts (as of April 2026)
- **Valuation:** ~$134B
- **Total raised:** $20B+
- **Revenue:** $5.4B
- **Origin:** Grew out of Zaharia's 2009 UC Berkeley PhD project, [[apache-spark]]
- **Current positioning:** Data foundation layer for AI workloads and agents — the substrate layer underneath the model/agent layer occupied by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google

## Why It Matters Here
- Databricks is the best living example of the "lineage from prior platform wave to current AI wave" pattern: big-data infra → AI data infra, same company, same founders. Relevant context for any strategic thinking about what Wayloft/PBP infrastructure could become.
- CTO Zaharia's public takes on AGI and agent security (April 2026 ACM Prize interview) are a direct data point for [[open-claw]] risk analysis.
