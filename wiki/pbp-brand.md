# Pickleball Portal Brand Guide

## Key Takeaways
- Tagline: "Your Window Into the World of Pickleball"
- Founded November 2017 — one of the first pickleball content sites
- "100% independent — no brand owns us, no publisher controls us"
- Primary color: Portal Green #22c55e. Dark Green: #104E29. Charcoal: #1a1a2e. Orange accent: #F97316.
- Typography: system sans-serif (Inter/Geist), 700-900 headlines, 400 body
- Icons: Lucide React ONLY. No emojis anywhere in UI/content.
- Real photos only — no AI-generated images for heroes, products, lifestyle
- "Window" metaphor is central, max once per page
- Affiliate disclosure framed as trust-building: "Independent & Unbiased"
- Voice: Authoritative, Independent, Passionate, Pioneering, Accessible, Transparent

## Naming Conventions
- Portal Score (not "our score" or "the algorithm")
- Paddle Finder (not "quiz" or "recommendation engine")
- Full Coverage (not "topic page")
- My Portal (not "dashboard")
- Price Tracker (not "deal finder")

## Competitive Positioning
- vs The Dink: newsletter + news vs tools + data + pricing
- vs Pickleball Kitchen: beginners vs all levels with deeper analysis
- vs JustPaddles: retailer vs independent cross-retailer comparison
- Moat: "Founded 2017 + DA 43 + AI tools + multi-retailer data + independence. Nobody else has all five."
