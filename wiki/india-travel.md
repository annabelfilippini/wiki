# India

Day-by-day itinerary covering Delhi, Agra, Pushkar, Goa, and Hampi.

## Itinerary

### Oct 29-30: Delhi + Taj Mahal
- Arrive Delhi, stay at Legends Inn
- **Oct 30:** Taxi to Agra (3 hr drive, 7am pickup). Tour includes luggage storage in van until 2pm
- Taj Mahal tickets: buy there (timing uncertainty), not online
- Train from Agra to Pushkar (leaves 2:50pm from Agra Cantt -- arrive early for foreigner booth tickets)

### Oct 31 - Nov 2: Pushkar Camel Festival
- Stay at Everest Hotel (3 nights)
- **Pushkar Lake** -- walk to Ranji Temple (very close)
- **Sunrise hike** to Savitari Mata Temple (15 min walk from lake)
- The camel festival is the main event

### Nov 2-4: Goa
- Taxi Pushkar to Jaipur airport, fly to Goa (11:50 flight, arrive ~6:30pm)
- Stay at Cozy Nook Hotel until Nov 5
- **Nov 4:** 8am yoga, 11am cooking class, 2:30pm rent bikes/moped to Palolem Beach

### Nov 5-8: Hampi
- 8:30pm bus from Goa, drops at Hospet (7km outside Hampi)
- Stay at Vicky's Homestay
- Cool architecture to explore
- Train from Hampi to Bengaluru for flight out

## Budget Snapshot
- Van to Taj Mahal: $50
- Pushkar hotel (3 nights): $40
