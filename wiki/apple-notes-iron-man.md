# Apple Notes: Half Ironman Training

## Key Takeaways
- Brick workouts (bike + swim back-to-back) are the key training method
- Tri bike fitting: forward lean on aero bars engages hamstrings/glutes, saves quads for run
- Nutrition: carb-load night before, gels + water on race day, mount gels on bike
- Practice transitions -- layout and speed matter
- Mental framing: "honored to be able to do this"
