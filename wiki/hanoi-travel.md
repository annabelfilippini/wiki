# Hanoi

Travel notes from visiting Hanoi, Vietnam.

## Must-Do
- **Train Street** -- lined with coffee shops; train passes at ~3:30pm and 7:30pm (not strictly on time)
- **Old Quarter** -- cute cafes, shops, night market (ideal place to stay)
- **Water Puppet Show** -- purchase audio guide (performance is in Vietnamese)
- **West Lake** -- explore shops on the water, see Tran Quoc Pagoda (temple)

## Cafes
- **Note Cafe** -- 4 floors, notes from customers on the walls
- **Loading Tea Cafe** -- recommended starting point for walking exploration
- **Egg Coffee** -- Hanoi specialty, must try

## Walking Route
Loading Tea Cafe -> Confucian Temple of Literature (through Old Quarter, cool artwork) -> West Lake Temple (Buddhist) -> Manzi Art Space (boutiques, cafes, quiet)

## Nightlife
- **Ta Hien Beer Street** -- street performers

## Wellness
- Head spa
- Nha Spa

## Food
- Bun Cha -- Hanoi signature dish
