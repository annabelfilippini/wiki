# Apple Notes: Travel India

## Key Takeaways
- 10-day itinerary: Delhi -> Taj Mahal -> Pushkar Camel Festival -> Goa -> Hampi -> Bengaluru
- Practical tips: buy Taj tickets on-site, arrive early at Agra Cantt for foreigner booth, bus from Goa drops at Hospet (7km from Hampi)
- Pushkar highlights: lake, Ranji Temple, sunrise hike to Savitari Mata Temple
