# The Year Ahead 2026: Optimism with Footnotes

Bill Gates' annual outlook essay, published January 9, 2026. Frames 2026 as a pivotal year where innovation (especially AI) creates long-term optimism, but near-term setbacks require "footnotes."

## Key Takeaways

- Child deaths under 5 rose from 4.6M (2024) to 4.8M (2025), the first increase this century, driven by reduced rich-country aid
- If health funding drops 20%, 12M more children could die by 2045
- AI will "change society the most" of anything humans have ever created
- Gates believes AI will exceed human-level intelligence, no plateau before that point
- Bioterrorism via open-source AI is now a greater risk than natural pandemics (echoes his 2015 TED talk warning)
- AI already makes software developers "at least twice as efficient"
- Job market disruption will grow significantly over the next 5 years
- World has cut projected emissions by 40%+ in the last decade through clean energy innovation

## Notable Claims

- Gates Foundation + OpenAI launched **Horizon1000**: $50M initiative for AI in African healthcare, starting in Rwanda, targeting 1,000 primary clinics by 2028
- Rwanda has 1 health worker per 1,000 people (WHO recommends ~4); at current pace, closing that gap would take 180 years
- Rwanda's Minister of Health Dr. Sabin Nsanzimana launched an AI-powered Health Intelligence Center in Kigali
- Gates Foundation committed $1.4B to support farmers on climate frontlines
- Alzheimer's diagnosis breakthrough will revolutionize testing and prevention
- AI can soon give poor farmers better advice on weather, prices, crop diseases, and soil than even the richest farmers get today

## Quotes

> "Of all the things humans have ever created, AI will change society the most."

> "There is no upper limit on how intelligent AIs will get or on how good robots will get, and I believe the advances will not plateau before exceeding human levels."

> "An even greater risk than a naturally caused pandemic is that a non-government group will use open source AI tools to design a bioterrorism weapon."

> "AI makes software developers at least twice as efficient, which makes coding cheaper while also creating demand elasticity for code."

## New Information

- First concrete data on 2025 child mortality regression (4.6M to 4.8M)
- Horizon1000 initiative details (Gates Foundation + OpenAI, $50M, Rwanda first)
- Gates' personal position that AI has no ceiling and will exceed human intelligence
- Framing of bioterrorism as the #1 existential risk above natural pandemics
- 40% emissions reduction figure as measure of clean energy progress
