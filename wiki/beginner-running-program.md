# Beginner Running Program

A 6-week program Annabel designed for a beginner runner, building from walk/jog intervals to a timed mile. Demonstrates progressive overload and coaching knowledge.

## Program Structure

| Week | Intervals | Walk | Push Run | Long Run |
|------|-----------|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | 45s jog / 45s walk x10 | 20 min | -- | 2 mi |
| 2 | 60s jog / 45s walk x10 | 25 min | 1.5 mi | 2.5 mi |
| 3 | 90s jog / 45s walk x10 | 30 min | 1.5 mi + hills | 2.5 mi |
| 4 | 120s jog / 45s walk x10 | 35 min | 2 mi + time trial | 3 mi |
| 5 | 120s jog / 45s walk x10 | 40 min | 1.5 mi push | 3 mi |
| 6 | 150s jog / 45s walk x10 | 40 min | hills (8 sprints) | Race day |

## Key Principles
- **Progressive overload:** Interval jog time increases from 45s to 150s across 6 weeks
- **Walk days are real training:** 20-40 min walks for active recovery
- **Two time trials** (weeks 1 and 4) to measure improvement
- **Hill sprints** introduced in weeks 3 and 6 for power
- **Long runs always easy** -- building endurance, not speed
- **Push runs are hard** -- "shouldn't be easy" -- separate from long runs
- **Bookend jogs:** Warm-up and cool-down jogs frame every interval session (5 min each)

## Coaching Voice
Direct, encouraging, specific. "KICK BUTT, your hard work is gonna pay off!!!!"
