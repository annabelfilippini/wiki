# Apple Notes: Lucy Training Schedule

## Key Takeaways
- 6-week beginner running program with progressive overload
- Walk/jog intervals increasing from 45s to 150s
- Two time trials (weeks 1 and 4) for progress measurement
- Hill sprints for power development
- Long runs always easy, push runs always hard
