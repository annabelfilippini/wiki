# World Models

AI systems designed to learn and simulate how the physical world operates, enabling prediction and decision-making about real-world environments. Distinct from LLMs (which model language) — world models aim to model physics, spatial relationships, and causality.

## How It Works
- Learn representations of physical environments from data (video, sensor input, simulation)
- Build internal models that predict what happens next in a physical scene
- Enable downstream tasks: robotics, autonomous driving, physical planning, simulation

## Why It Matters
- Represents a paradigm beyond text-based AI — moving toward systems that "understand" the physical world
- Key enabler for robotics and embodied AI
- Alexandre LeBrun (CEO of [[ami-labs]]) predicts "world models" will become the next AI buzzword — suggesting both genuine importance and incoming hype cycle
