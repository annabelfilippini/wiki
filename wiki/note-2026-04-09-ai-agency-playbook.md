# Note — AI-Powered Agency Playbook (Agency as R&D for Agent SaaS)

## Key Takeaways
- **Productized agency thesis is back.** The 2022 productized agency model failed because scaling humans was the bottleneck — inconsistent output, turnover, margin compression. AI (Claude Code, OpenClaw, etc.) solves the labor problem, making the model viable again with 80%+ margins.
- **Agency = paid R&D for SaaS.** The core insight: run a retainer-based agency ($3-5k/month per client) where every engagement teaches you what to automate. By month 4, you know exactly what to productize. Clients fund your software development and become your first SaaS customers.
- **The financial model is concrete.** Year 1: 10 clients at $4k/month = $480k revenue, ~$400k take-home (2 people). Year 2: launch software at $800/month, agency clients convert first. Year 3: 200 software customers = $1.9M ARR, 85% margins, $10-15M exit at 5-8x revenue.
- **Capital efficiency is the argument.** Zero VC, $0 startup cost, 2-3 person team, 3-year path to a software exit. Framed as "the most capital efficient path to a software exit that exists right now."
- **Pick one deliverable, one buyer.** Not "marketing" — something specific like "SEO content for e-commerce brands doing $1M+" or "ad creatives for DTC brands spending $50k/month on Meta." Narrow focus is load-bearing.
