# AMI Labs $1.03B Funding

## Key Takeaways
- [[ami-labs]] raised $1.03B to build [[world-models]] — AI systems that understand and predict physical environments
- [[yann-lecun]] serves as Executive Chairman; Alexandre LeBrun is CEO
- LeBrun himself predicts "world models" will become the next overhyped AI buzzword within six months

## Notable Claims
- World models represent a distinct AI category from LLMs — focused on simulating physical world dynamics rather than language
- The funding size ($1.03B) signals serious institutional belief in the world models paradigm
- LeBrun's self-aware skepticism about the term becoming a buzzword is notable coming from someone raising a billion dollars on the concept

## Quotes
> "My prediction is that 'world models' will be the next buzzword. In six months, every company will call itself a world model to raise funding." — Alexandre LeBrun, CEO
