# Vannevar Bush's Memex

A hypothetical personal knowledge device described by Vannevar Bush in his 1945 essay "As We May Think." The Memex was envisioned as a desk-sized device that stores all of a person's books, records, and communications, with the ability to create "associative trails" — chains of linked documents that mirror how humans actually think.

## Connection to LLM Wiki

Bush's vision was closer to the [[llm-wiki]] pattern than to what the web became:
- **Private and curated** — not public, not crowd-sourced
- **Associative trails** — the connections between documents are as valuable as the documents themselves
- **Actively maintained** — the user builds up trails over time

The part Bush couldn't solve: who does the maintenance? Creating and maintaining associative trails is labor-intensive. The LLM handles that, making the Memex vision practically achievable for the first time.
