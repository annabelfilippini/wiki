# Apple Notes: Pickleball Portal Notes

## Key Takeaways
- **Paddle thickness:** Thicker = more control (given to beginners), thinner = less control (advanced)
- **USA Pickleball guidelines:** 12mm to 16mm paddle thickness
- Paddles must be USA Pickleball approved -- no holes except Vagabond's single bottom hole
- Circular paddles claim to reduce vibration/arthritis -- pro (Brad) is skeptical, potential myth-bust content
- **Handle length:** Longer handles for two-handed players
- **Mold types:** Single-mold (one piece) vs handle+paddle molded together
- **Top brands:** Selkirk (has ambassador "Project Lab" program) and Joola have best reputation currently
- Superfly -- brand promoted by Gates community

First-hand pro interview data. Brad (pickleball pro at Gates) is a primary source. Updates [[pickleball-portal]] with field research.
