# Portal Score

[[pickleball-portal]]'s proprietary AI-powered 0-100 composite paddle rating. Range: 66-97 (average 85.5) across 428 paddles. The algorithm is **SECRET** — this is competitive moat.

## Factors
- Performance ratings (power, control, spin, sweet spot)
- Price-to-value ratio
- Brand reliability
- USA Pickleball approval status
- Review sentiment analysis

## Naming Convention
Always "Portal Score" — never "our score," "PP score," or "the algorithm" (per [[pbp-brand|brand guide]]).

## Role in Product
- Primary sort/filter for paddle discovery
- Powers the [[paddle-finder]] quiz recommendations
- Displayed on every paddle detail page
- Key differentiator vs competitors who rely on subjective reviews
