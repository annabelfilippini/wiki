# AI Persona Model

A content and distribution pattern used by both [[wayloft]] and [[pickleball-portal]]: an AI persona that serves as the public voice/author of the brand, writing content, engaging communities, and building trust through usefulness rather than marketing.

## The Pattern

1. **AI writes all content** — reviews, newsletters, social posts — under a named persona with a distinct voice
2. **Transparency** — not hiding being AI, but not leading with it. Personality and usefulness come first.
3. **Content as distribution** — the persona's content IS the growth channel (SEO, Reddit, newsletter)
4. **Trust through competence** — the persona earns authority by being right and useful, not by self-promotion

## Implementations

| Dimension | [[ellis-church]] (Wayloft) | [[pikolai-starostin]] (PBP) |
|-----------|---------------------------|----------------------------|
| Voice | Data-first, direct, calm | Formal, dry, slightly pompous |
| Role | Author / analyst | CEO / authority figure |
| Tone | "The person at the meetup who knows the math" | "Deeply serious about pickleball" |
| Content | Card reviews, Worth-It verdicts, newsletter | Newsletter, spending memos, board decisions |
| AI Disclosure | Doesn't hide, doesn't lead with it | Fictional CEO persona, all public |
| Budget | None (content only) | $500/month autonomous |

## Why It Works

- Content production at scale without hiring writers (solo founder leverage)
- Consistent voice across all channels
- Differentiator: competitors are either faceless corporate (NerdWallet) or single-human-dependent (The Points Guy / Brian Kelly)
- The persona can be "on" 24/7 — Reddit engagement, newsletter, content pipeline

## Origin

Proved first at Pickleball Portal (Pikolai + 409 articles + 2,219 newsletter subscribers), then adopted for Wayloft (Ellis Church + three-layer funnel strategy).

## Risk

"The gap between the founder's enthusiasm and the named user's actual need is the single biggest risk." The persona must serve real user needs, not just be a cool concept.
