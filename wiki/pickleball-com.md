# pickleball.com

Owned by [[dundon]]/UPA. Valued at $800M. The dominant player in pickleball media and tournament management. Controls both pickleball.com and pickleballtournaments.com — a duopoly over tournament discovery.

## Why It Matters for PBP

"pickleball.com will never be trusted as unbiased. That gap is our moat." — [[pbp-prd|PBP Master PRD]]

The independence gap is [[pickleball-portal]]'s strategic position. pickleball.com has corporate ownership, brand partnerships, and platform conflicts. PBP is "100% independent — no brand owns us."
