# Yann LeCun

Pioneering AI researcher. Turing Award winner (2018) for work on deep learning. VP and Chief AI Scientist at Meta. Executive Chairman of [[ami-labs]].

## Key Facts
- **Role at Meta:** VP & Chief AI Scientist
- **Role at AMI Labs:** Executive Chairman
- **Recognition:** Turing Award (2018, shared with Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio)
- **Focus:** Advocate for [[world-models]] and self-supervised learning as the path to more capable AI, critical of pure autoregressive LLM approaches
