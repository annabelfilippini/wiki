# California Guide

Lived-in local knowledge for the Bay Area and beyond. Compiled from time spent in Berkeley and exploring the region.

## Berkeley

### Running
- **Fire trails** -- also a sunset spot
- **The Marina** -- flat waterfront running

### Coffee
- Cafe Strata, Baker and Common, Souvenir, High Wire, Caffeinated
- **SoDol** -- French toast latte

### Food
- **Boichik Bagels** -- best bagels in CA (breakfast)
- **Cheese Board** -- dinner
- **Italian Homemade** -- quick dinners
- **Mezzo** -- lunch
- **Fentons** -- ice cream

### Neighborhoods
- Elmwood, North Berkeley

## Marin County

### Hikes
- Tennessee Valley, Phoenix Lake (Yolanda Trail Loop), Bald Hill
- **Matt Davis Trail** to Bootjack
- **West Blithedale Trail** -- endless trails
- **Dipsea Trail** all the way to Stinson Beach
- Bass Lake, Bolinas Ridge drive

### Food
- **COMFORTS** -- Chicken Okasan
- Rustic Bakery (chicken salad)
- Fish in Sausalito
- Pizzeria Picco in Larkspur
- Mill Valley Lumberyard
- **Hog Island Oyster Company** -- picnic spot

### Activities
- Love Story Yoga, Body Rock

## Surfing
- **Linda Mar / Pacifica** -- small waves, check Shelter Cove Beach (hidden)
- **Bolinas** -- favorite spot (at least do the drive via Point Reyes)
- **Montara Beach**

## Day Trips from Berkeley
- **SF:** BART to Mission for burritos, Dolores Park, Fillmore Street bars (Jaxon, Balboa Cafe, Mauna Loa)
- **Alameda Flea Market** -- 600+ vendors, first Sunday of every month at 8am
- **Greek Theater** -- summer concerts
- **Hiking:** Tilden, Muir Woods, Point Reyes (must-do)
- **Walnut Creek**

## Beyond the Bay
- **Mendocino** -- cute town
- **Carmel** -- 17 Mile Drive
- **San Diego:** North County (Del Mar, Carlsbad)
- **Point Reyes** -- don't leave without going
