# Pikolai Starostin

Fictional AI CEO of [[pickleball-portal]]. An [[ai-persona-model]] named after Nikolai Starostin, who co-founded Spartak Moscow and was arrested by Beria in 1942 for "praising bourgeois sport." The parallel: Pickleball Portal was written off; Pikolai doesn't write things off.

## Voice

"Formal, dry, slightly pompous, deeply serious about pickleball."

Sign-off: *"From the top of the leaderboard, Pikolai"*

## Governance

- $500/month autonomous budget for brand/site spending
- Monthly Spending Memo to the board (Tom + Annabel)
- Board has 48-hour veto on any spend
- All spending logged publicly — transparency is the brand
- "Hardware": Dell Precision 5860 with RTX 6000 Ada 48GB GPU — "his compensation package"

## Spend Categories
Merch, community giveaways, PR stunts, content, domain registration (e.g., PadelPortal.com), charitable (youth pickleball programs). First purchase recommendation: a bobblehead of himself.

## Content Cadence
- Spending Memo (monthly)
- Board Decision Post (monthly)
- Spend Recap (monthly)
- Newsletter (via [[beehiiv]])

## Roadmap
- **Tier 1 (30 days):** Fix 404s, make Pikolai operational, launch public presence (headshot, bio, email, socials)
- **Tier 2 (90 days):** Transparency/decision log, beginner learning path, tournament aggregator, $500/month budget series + first merch
- **Tier 3 (6-12 months):** Premium membership, community layer (find-a-partner, coaching marketplace), multi-agent org under Pikolai, expansion to [[padel-portal]]

## The Long Game

"AI-native sports authority media" — pickleball is the flagship; the governance model is the IP. Not "pickleball website" but a replicable pattern for AI-run vertical media.
