# Affiliate Revenue Model

Primary monetization for both [[wayloft]] and [[pickleball-portal]]. The shared philosophy: "We test and rate FIRST, monetize SECOND." Trust > affiliate revenue.

## Wayloft

- Revenue split target: affiliates 55-65%, subscriptions 25-30%
- Research shows affiliates yield 2-3x subscription revenue
- Primary networks: CardRatings, CJ (Commission Junction), FlexOffers, Impact
- Affiliate rejection contingency: Amazon Associates for travel gear, Skimlinks as catch-all
- Reapply to premium networks after 5,000 monthly page views
- [[ellis-church]] editorial rule: "Never shill. If a card isn't worth it, say so."

## Pickleball Portal

- Current (Amazon only): ~$80/mo on 210 clicks (March 2026)
- Total potential with all tiers: ~$1,000+/mo on 428 paddles at 100K visitors
- **Active:** Amazon Associates (tag: pickleball07a-20), Skimlinks (48K+ merchants, 25% cut)
- **Applied (April 3):** Selkirk (15% via AvantLink), JOOLA (10-12% via Awin), CRBN (10-15% via Refersion)
- **Dead:** JustPaddles (all links removed), Genius Links (declared dead Apr 4 — zero code integration despite existing account at daniellangston10@gmail.com)
- GA4 affiliate click tracking deployed
- Brand guide framing: "Independent & Unbiased" disclosure as trust-building

## Shared Pattern

Both products position independence as the moat. Wayloft differentiates from The Points Guy (affiliate-driven content); PBP differentiates from JustPaddles (retailer) and brand blogs (Selkirk, JOOLA). The credibility of "we'll tell you when something isn't worth it" IS the affiliate business model — trust drives clicks.
