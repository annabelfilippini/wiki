# Neuralink

Elon Musk's [[brain-computer-interface]] company. Developing implantable chips for thought-to-action control.

## Key Facts
- Founded by Elon Musk
- Conducting human clinical trials in the US
- Has demonstrated thought-to-text and cursor control in trial participants
- As of March 2026, still in clinical trial phase -- China beat them to first regulatory approval for non-trial use ([[china-approves-brain-chip]])
