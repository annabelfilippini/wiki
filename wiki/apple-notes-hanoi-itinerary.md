# Apple Notes: Hanoi Itinerary

## Key Takeaways
- Train Street (trains at ~3:30pm and 7:30pm), Note Cafe, Loading Tea Cafe, West Lake temples
- Water puppet show needs audio guide (Vietnamese language)
- Egg coffee is the must-try local specialty
- Head spa and Nha Spa for wellness
