# China Approves Brain Chip to Treat Paralysis -- A World First

## Key Takeaways
- China has approved a [[brain-computer-interface]] (BCI) implant for people with severe paralysis to restore hand movements
- The chip allows users to control a soft robotic hand using brain signals
- **First BCI in the world approved for use outside clinical trials** -- a regulatory milestone
- Published in *Nature* 651, 865-866 (2026)

## Notable Claims
- This is a world first in terms of regulatory approval for non-trial BCI use
- The implant specifically targets hand movement restoration in paralysis patients

## New Information
- China, not the US, achieved the first regulatory approval for commercial BCI use -- notable given [[neuralink]]'s high-profile US trials
- Signals a shift in BCI from experimental to clinical reality
