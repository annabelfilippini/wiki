# Half Ironman Training

Notes from preparing for a Half Ironman (70.3). Training approach, bike fitting for triathlon, and race-day strategy.

## Training Structure
- **Monday:** Full distance training day
- **2x per week:** 20-30 mile bike + 2-3 mile run (brick workout)
- **Bike rides:** Target ~3 hours

## Brick Workouts
The key triathlon-specific training -- back-to-back disciplines so the body learns to recover between transitions.
- Ride to lake, then swim 30 min
- Teaches the body to switch from one discipline to the next

## Bike Fitting for Triathlon
- Get fitted at a bike shop -- specify it's for a triathlon
- **Tri bars (aero bars)** change body position to lean forward
- This engages **hamstrings and glutes** instead of burning out quads
- Critical for saving legs for the run after the bike

## Transitions
- Transitions take real time -- need to practice layout
- Figure out if the race is point-to-point or single transition area
- Practice: glasses placement, gear layout, speed of changeover

## Nutrition Strategy
- **Night before:** Carb-load
- **Race day:** Gels + water + electrolytes
- Mount gels on bike frame
- Start eating gels when mouth gets dry (sign of dehydration/exertion)
- Start practicing gel nutrition in training, not just on race day

## Mental Preparation
- Study the course maps and visualize the path
- "I'm honored to be able to do this" -- frame it as joyful, not just hard
- Race locations considered: Oceanside, Napa
