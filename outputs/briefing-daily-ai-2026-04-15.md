# Morning Briefing — 2026-04-15

## AI Tools, Tech & Advancements

1. **Google Gemma 4 — Open Source Model That Runs Offline** — Released April 2 under Apache 2.0, Gemma 4's 31B dense model is beating rivals 10x its size on reasoning benchmarks, and the whole family (2B to 31B) runs fully on consumer hardware — phones, Raspberry Pis, consumer GPUs. Native vision and audio, up to 256K context, fluent in 140+ languages. Devs are buzzing because it's genuinely fast at agentic tasks offline, and it's commercially permissive. Already 400M+ cumulative downloads across the Gemma line and 100K+ community variants. ([Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/gemma/gemma-4/))

2. **NotebookLM Is Now Inside Gemini** — Google fully merged NotebookLM into the Gemini app on April 10. You can now build knowledge notebooks directly inside Gemini — upload PDFs, paste URLs, add YouTube videos — and chat over them without leaving the interface. Notebooks sync bidirectionally; unique NotebookLM features (Audio Overviews, Video Overviews, Infographics) stay available. Rolling out to paid subscribers first (AI Ultra/Pro/Plus), then free users. This is the closest a major AI assistant has come to a built-in second brain — directly relevant to the Second Brain product direction. ([Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/04/10/google-integrates-notebooklm-into-gemini-app-for-all-users/))

3. **OpenClaw — 250K GitHub Stars in 4 Months** — OpenClaw, an open-source project started by Austrian developer Peter Steinberger in late 2025, has crossed 250,000 GitHub stars in under four months, making it one of the fastest-rising repos in recent memory. It's gaining traction in the local AI community as a developer-first alternative framework for running and composing agentic workflows. The speed of community adoption suggests real practitioner demand, not just hype. Worth watching. ([Reddit/r/LocalLLaMA discussion](https://dev.to/b1fe7066aefjbingbong/reddits-most-upvoted-ai-tools-of-2026-ranked-3hhl))

4. **Claude Mythos + Project Glasswing — Cybersecurity-Only AI** — Anthropic quietly previewed Claude Mythos on April 7, a specialized model built specifically to find and fix severe software vulnerabilities. It won't be released publicly — too dangerous. It's available only to vetted security partners under "Project Glasswing." The model's existence confirms the trajectory: increasingly powerful frontier models getting siloed into high-trust, high-liability verticals rather than open access. This is the template for how dangerous capability gets deployed going forward. ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/bad-news-claude-users-anthropic-says-youll-need-to-pay-to-use-openclaw-now))

5. **Kimi's 2M Token Context — Quietly Dominant** — Moonshot AI's Kimi model is circulating in practitioner circles for one reason: a 2 million token context window, the longest among commercial AIs. That's enough to ingest an entire codebase, a company's full document library, or years of conversation history in a single prompt. No dramatic launch, no viral demo — just devs quietly switching for long-context use cases. Worth knowing about given the Second Brain use case. ([AI Tool Discovery](https://www.aitooldiscovery.com/guides/best-ai-tools-reddit))

## AI Industry News & Shifts

1. **OpenAI Raises $122B at $852B Valuation — Stargate Is Real** — OpenAI closed a record $122 billion funding round last week and has completed its restructuring into a Public Benefit Corporation. The Stargate campus in Abilene, TX is live at 1.2 GW of Oracle Cloud infrastructure, with 7 GW of planned capacity across Michigan, Wisconsin, Wyoming, Pennsylvania, and Texas. SoftBank injected a fresh $10B infusion this month. For context: this is more compute infrastructure than most countries have ever built. The question is no longer whether AGI-scale infrastructure will be built — it's whether the grid can handle it. Sam Altman's public concern: "If there are no major breakthroughs in energy technology, AI will not reach its next stage." ([Data Center Knowledge](https://www.datacenterknowledge.com/infrastructure/openai-raises-122b-to-expand-ai-infrastructure-broadens-cloud-and-chip-strategy))

2. **Big Three Form Coalition Against Chinese Model Cloning** — OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google announced April 6-7 that they're sharing intelligence through the Frontier Model Forum to stop adversarial distillation from Chinese AI firms. Anthropic specifically named DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, and MiniMax, claiming those three generated over 16 million exchanges with Claude via ~24,000 fraudulent API accounts to extract training signal. This is the first formal industry-wide defensive coalition of its kind. It also signals that model differentiation is real enough to steal — and that stolen capability is closing the frontier gap faster than anyone expected. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-06/openai-anthropic-google-unite-to-combat-model-copying-in-china))

3. **Anthropic Moves Toward Custom Chips** — Anthropic is in early-stage discussions about designing its own AI chips, joining Meta and OpenAI in pushing for independence from NVIDIA. The company already expanded its partnership with Google and Broadcom for next-gen compute. OpenAI is further along: it's designing custom accelerators with Broadcom deploying from late 2026 through 2029 and now runs a mixed-silicon stack including AMD, AWS Trainium, Cerebras, and custom ASICs. The NVIDIA moat is cracking from all sides simultaneously. ([ResultSense](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-04-10-anthropic-explores-designing-own-ai-chips))

4. **"Great Recession for White-Collar Workers" — No Longer Hypothetical** — Anthropic published research in March mapping out which jobs AI could replace, and Fortune called a "Great Recession for white-collar workers" explicitly possible. Microsoft AI chief Mustafa Suleyman put a number on it: most professional knowledge work automated within 18 months. HBR found companies are already laying off workers based on AI's *potential*, not its actual current performance — a self-fulfilling dynamic. The jobs discourse has shifted from "maybe" to "when." For Annabel starting at Okta: AI-native skills are already commanding 56% wage premiums over equivalent non-AI roles. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/03/06/ai-job-losses-report-anthropic-research-great-recession-for-white-collar-workers/))

## World News

1. **US-Iran Ceasefire Holding — Barely** — The two-week US-Iran ceasefire is in its final days, with Trump telling the New York Post that fresh talks could resume "over the next two days" via Pakistan as intermediary. But satellite images reviewed by CNN show Iran has been clearing debris from underground missile base entrances during the ceasefire window, raising questions about whether Tehran is preparing for renewed conflict or simply maintaining facilities. The Strait of Hormuz has partially reopened, but shipping insurance hesitancy means energy flows remain well below normal. Both sides are pointing to the other's preconditions as obstacles to extending the ceasefire. ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/04/14/world/live-news/iran-war-blockade-us-trump))

2. **IMF Cuts Global Growth Forecast, Blames Hormuz** — The IMF released its World Economic Outlook on April 14, cutting global 2026 growth to 3.1% (down from 3.4% in 2025). The Middle East and Central Asia region was slashed by 2 full percentage points to 1.9%. Headline inflation is now forecast at 4.4%, driven by energy prices. US growth holds at 2.3%; Eurozone slides to 1.1%. The ECB president Christine Lagarde separately warned that war-related economic damage will be long-lasting even after a ceasefire, given infrastructure destruction along key shipping corridors. ([IMF](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/weo/issues/2026/04/14/world-economic-outlook-april-2026) / [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/4/14/imf-cuts-global-growth-forecast-during-hormuz-blockade))

3. **Trump Tariffs — Largest Tax Increase Since 1993** — The Tax Foundation puts Trump's 2026 tariffs at the largest US tax increase as a share of GDP since 1993, averaging $1,500 per US household this year. The peak impact window is April through October. On top of baseline tariffs, Trump threatened 50% duties on any country supplying military weapons to Iran — analysts called it legally dubious, but the signal is clear: tariffs are now geopolitical leverage tools, not just trade policy. China is the primary target given reported plans for arms shipment to Iran. ([Tax Foundation](https://taxfoundation.org/research/all/federal/trump-tariffs-trade-war/) / [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/13/trump-threatens-50percent-tariffs-on-china-as-report-suggests-plans-for-arms-shipment-to-iran.html))

4. **Sudan: 80% of Displaced Families Skipping Meals** — The humanitarian situation in Sudan has reached critical levels, with more than 80% of displaced families skipping meals to survive and 18% sending children to work. The conflict that escalated in 2023 has produced one of the world's worst humanitarian crises with limited international media coverage relative to its scale. Aid organizations are warning of famine conditions in multiple regions. ([Vindicator via wire services](https://www.vindy.com/news/national-news/2026/04/nation-and-world-at-a-glance-for-april-15/))
