# Weekly Opportunity Scan — 2026-05-11

## Top 5

1. **Loyalty Devaluation Alerter** — Wayloft-native alert system for award chart changes, transfer ratio cuts, and annual-fee increases. Monitor public program pages + community signals; email alert 30+ days before effective date. Desperate user: any points holder blindsided by Cathay Pacific's third devaluation in 12 months (May 1, zero advance warning). Distribution: Wayloft three-layer funnel → FlyerTalk → travel blogs. ([Cathay Pacific devaluation reaction](https://milelion.com/2026/05/03/full-details-cathay-pacifics-asia-miles-devaluation/)) ([AwardWallet 2FA breakdown](https://awardwallet.com/blog/balance-watch/))

2. **New Grad Total Compensation Decoder** — Paste your offer letter; get a plain-English breakdown of RSU tax treatment at vest, HSA vs FSA trade-offs, 401k match timing, and benefit start dates, ranked by dollar impact. Desperate user: Class of 2026 entering first corporate job (Annabel's exact cohort), too embarrassed to ask HR, too confused to Google 15 separate questions. Distribution: LinkedIn (massive cohort in May–August), TikTok finance, r/cscareerquestions. No competitor found (week 4). ([RSU/401k confusion confirmed acute](https://www.faangfire.com/p/2026-rsu-dashboard-and-total-compensation)) ([Benefits confusion context](https://www.paychex.com/articles/employee-benefits/new-hire-open-enrollment))

3. **Regulatory Pulse** — Automated monitor for specific regulatory pages (US employment law, GDPR/CCPA, tax thresholds). When a page changes, send a Slack/email alert with an AI-generated plain-English summary of what changed and what action is required. Desperate user: 5–50 person startup that can't afford GRC tools or legal retainers but faces real compliance obligations. Distribution: Hacker News (someone in the May "What Are You Working On" thread is literally building this, validating demand organically). Unit economics: $49–99/month per company, high retention (compliance is non-discretionary). ([HN May 2026 thread — change monitoring project](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47997458)) ([Regulatory monitoring is mandatory by design in 2026](https://celestix.com/why-regulatory-monitoring-must-be-automated-by-design-in-2026/))

4. **AI Displacement Career Pivot Plan** — Not a resume builder. A 90-day repositioning plan: audit which % of weekly tasks are AI-automatable, identify pivot paths within your existing domain (not "learn to code"), generate week-by-week action items. Desperate user: mid-career PM, analyst, or content role who hasn't been laid off yet but sees the Goldman 16K jobs/month data and wants a proactive plan while still employed. Metaintro produces free blog content on this; no one has built the actual tool. Distribution: LinkedIn (everyone posting about AI anxiety), $99 one-time plan generation. ([Metaintro AI displacement survival playbook](https://www.metaintro.com/blog/ai-displacement-survival-playbook-2026)) ([37% of business leaders plan worker replacement by end of 2026](https://www.designrush.com/agency/ai-companies/trends/ai-job-displacement-statistics))

5. **Managed LLM Wiki** — Hosted, managed version of the Karpathy second-brain setup for knowledge workers who can't configure Obsidian + Claude Code themselves. Ingest documents, maintain cross-references, answer queries against your own knowledge graph. Desperate user: indie founders, consultants, and researchers who want a compounding knowledge base but lack the technical setup time. Market: $2.16B growing at 30% CAGR. Kanwas (YC-backed open-source team KB) launching validates adjacent space; consumer personal KB remains unserved. Distribution: HN, tech Twitter. Warning: maintenance burden is high for a solo founder with a full-time job — this wants to be a standalone business, not a side project. ([Second brain market $2.16B at 30% CAGR](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/6226503/personal-knowledge-base-ai-market-report)) ([Kanwas — YC team KB launch](https://www.producthunt.com/products/kanwas))

## Killed This Week

- **Creator economy tools** — FocuSee 2.0, InVideo, Opus Clip, HeyGen all shipping fast. Crowded, established players. No white space for a solo founder entry.
- **Community Digest Engine** — Substack at 8.4M paid subs (+68% YoY), beehiiv at 1.2M active creators. Newsletter platform wall is real. No entry angle.
- **Construction WIP Automation** — No new validation. No fit with Annabel's distribution strengths. Dropped.
- **B2B Case Study Generator** — AthenaHQ and ProductLed expanding into this. Window closed.
- **Generic Startup Compliance Calendar** — Enterprise GRC players (Vanta, ServiceNow) closing the gap from above. Regulatory Pulse survives only as a niche-vertical play targeting sub-50-person companies.
- **Startup SaaS Renewal Tracker** — Ramp and Brex expanding into this tier; 6–12 month window has shortened to months. Killed.
- **Agentic Workflow Builder (meta)** — Gumloop, Dify, Taskade are general-purpose and well-funded. The opportunity is vertical-specific agents, not another orchestration layer.

## Watch List

- **Kanwas** (open-source team KB, YC-backed) — Launching on Product Hunt in May as top-voted product. Validates "AI brain for organizations" at YC scale. Adds pressure to Second Brain window — the longer Annabel waits, the more the space fills from the team side.
- **Solo unicorn wave** — Dario Amodei 70–80% probability of first solo unicorn in 2026. Pieter Levels ($3M+ ARR, zero employees), Marc Lou ($1M+ ARR) are the templates. This isn't a product opportunity — it's validation that the architecture Annabel is building (autonomous system + compounding wiki) is exactly right.
- **CardPointers MCP integration** — March 2026: CardPointers added Claude + ChatGPT + MCP integration, Apple Intelligence AutoPilot via Live Activities. This is the most concrete Wayloft AI-positioning threat yet. CardPointers is now an AI-first card optimizer with 2M+ users. Wayloft needs its MCP answer defined.
- **YC S26 batch** — 77 builders launching on Product Hunt with 60% of companies being AI-focused. Vertical agents and AI-native workflow replacements are where the smart money is going. Wayloft's "vertical travel AI" positioning is the right category.

## Kill / Build Signal

**Wayloft — STRONG BUILD (4th consecutive week):**
- Cathay Pacific devalued for the third time in 12 months (effective May 1). Community furious. Redemption rush confirmed. Zero predictive alert tool in market.
- AwardWallet Balance Watch is broken for most major programs (2FA blocks background sync). Their blog covers devaluations manually — the automation gap is real and uncontested.
- CardPointers MCP + Apple Intelligence = Wayloft's AI angle is now under direct threat. Wayloft needs to define its MCP answer this month, not next quarter. CardPointers' moat is iOS-native + 2M users. Wayloft's moat must be data depth + Ellis Church trust, not generic AI Q&A.
- RECOMMENDED ACTION: Ship the Devaluation Alerter as a free Wayloft feature (monitors 10 major programs) before June 1 Aeroplan changes. It's a marketing wedge, not a revenue line.

**Second Brain — SIGNAL HOLDS:**
- Market at $2.16B and growing at 30% CAGR confirms window is open.
- Kanwas launching adds pressure — the team/enterprise side is getting served by well-funded players. Consumer side is still open. Clock is ticking.

**Community Engine — NO SIGNAL (3rd consecutive week):**
- Deprioritize. The newsletter/community platform wall is real. No organic pain point surfaced in any channel this week.
