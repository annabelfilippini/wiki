# Morning Briefing — 2026-05-08

## AI Tools, Tech & Advancements

1. **OpenAI Codex + GPT-5.5: Parallel Agents in Isolated Worktrees** — OpenAI shipped GPT-5.5 in late April and it's now fully powering Codex, hitting 82.7% on Terminal-Bench 2.0 — the strongest agentic coding score posted by any model to date. The feature developers are most excited about: Codex now spins up multiple agents simultaneously, each working in an isolated git worktree to prevent merge conflicts, then merges results back. That's a real workflow change, not a demo. Developer Twitter is treating this as the moment agentic coding became production infrastructure, not a party trick. ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/) | [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/openai-codex-gpt-5-5-ai-agents/))

2. **Kimi's 2 Million Token Context Window** — China's Moonshot AI is picking up serious practitioner attention for Kimi, which offers a 2 million token context window — the longest of any commercial AI as of May 2026. The practical use case getting people excited: dropping entire codebases or document archives into a single prompt and querying across all of it. Worth watching if you're building knowledge-retrieval products — this context size starts to compete with vector databases for certain workloads. ([AI Tool Discovery](https://www.aitooldiscovery.com/guides/best-ai-tools-reddit))

3. **Microsoft Agent 365 Goes Live** — Microsoft launched Agent 365 on May 1 as a dedicated enterprise control plane for managing AI agents across an organization: provisioning, monitoring, access control, approvals. It's less a product people are excited about and more one they'll be forced to use — it's Microsoft's move to own the governance layer before anyone else does. Relevant for Annabel given the Okta overlap: identity and access management for agents is becoming its own category, and Okta is almost certainly watching this closely. ([DEV Community](https://dev.to/b1fe7066aefjbingbong/reddits-most-upvoted-ai-tools-of-2026-ranked-3hhl))

4. **Hachigo: Plain-Language to Workflow** — Quiet buzz on Product Hunt and Hacker News for Hachigo, a new tool that turns plain-language descriptions into executable multi-step workflows without code. It sits in the same space as Zapier + AI but with a more natural-language-first UX. Still early, but "describe what you want, get a working automation" is the interface pattern everyone's trying to crack right now and Hachigo is one of the cleaner attempts at it. ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products))

## AI Industry News & Shifts

1. **OpenAI and Anthropic Both Launch Enterprise Consulting JVs — On the Same Day** — On May 4, OpenAI and Anthropic each announced private-equity-backed AI services ventures targeting the $375B consulting industry, in what looks like a coordinated strike. Anthropic's version is backed by Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman, and Goldman Sachs; OpenAI's by TPG, Brookfield, and Bain at a $10B valuation. Both models embed AI engineers directly inside client organizations. The implication: the AI labs aren't just selling APIs anymore — they're competing with McKinsey, Accenture, and Deloitte for the implementation work. For enterprise software companies (hi, Okta), this is the moment to think hard about what sits between labs and clients. ([Nerd Level Tech](https://nerdleveltech.com/openai-anthropic-private-equity-consulting-ventures) | [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/05/anthropic-ceo-cyber-moment-of-danger-mythos-vulnerabilities.html))

2. **Anthropic's Mythos Model Is a Cybersecurity Crisis, Not Just a Product Launch** — Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei warned this week that Mythos — the company's unreleased frontier model — has already found thousands of high-severity vulnerabilities across every major OS and browser, and that China's models are "maybe six to 12 months" behind. The window to patch before adversarial models reach the same capability is roughly that long. About 40 institutions have access to Mythos to harden their systems; most governments and central banks do not — the EU is currently locked out while the US restricts its international rollout. This is the first time a lab has framed a model release primarily as a defensive security tool, and it's reshaping how regulators and banks think about AI risk. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/05/anthropic-ceo-cyber-moment-of-danger-mythos-vulnerabilities.html) | [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-04/eu-in-talks-with-anthropic-to-get-banks-tested-for-mythos-flaws))

3. **US Government Will Now Test AI Models Before Public Release** — The Center for AI Standards and Innovation announced agreements with Google DeepMind, Microsoft, and Elon Musk's xAI this week allowing US government evaluators to test AI models before they go live. This is the Trump administration's version of AI oversight: voluntary pre-deployment testing rather than mandatory compliance frameworks, but with major lab buy-in. OpenAI and Anthropic are notably absent from the announcement. The direction of travel is clear — government wants a seat at the table before frontier models ship, and the labs with military contracts are the first to sign on. ([CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/05/tech/microsoft-google-xai-government-test-ai-models) | [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/05/ai-oversight-trump-google-microsoft-xai.html))

4. **OpenAI at $25B ARR, Eyes Late-2026 IPO** — OpenAI has surpassed $25B in annualized revenue and is reportedly taking early steps toward a public listing, potentially as soon as late 2026. Anthropic is at $19B ARR. For context: a quarter of Google's and Amazon's "blowout AI profits" in Q1 came not from their own AI products but from paper gains on their Anthropic stakes — a sign of how much the market is pricing in future AI dominance rather than current revenue. An OpenAI IPO would be the biggest tech listing since Meta, and it would force the market to actually price the underlying business rather than the hype. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/30/google-amazon-ai-profits-anthropic-stake-bubble-earnings-2026/) | [blockchain.news](https://blockchain.news/ainews/anthropic-and-openai-dominate-2026-ai-race))

## World News

1. **Iran-US: Ceasefire Fragile, Strait Still Closed, Pakistan Mediating** — As of May 8, the Strait of Hormuz remains effectively closed — no commercial transits recorded since May 4, with roughly 1,600 ships stranded. The UAE reported its air defenses actively engaging missile and drone attacks overnight Thursday, and Iran says it struck US Navy ships after US forces targeted an Iranian tanker in Iranian territorial waters. Trump launched "Operation Project Freedom" on May 4 to escort merchant vessels through the strait, paused it May 6 citing "great progress" toward a deal, but the blockade remains. Pakistan is mediating a one-page memorandum that would declare an end to hostilities and trigger 30 days of nuclear/Hormuz negotiations — both sides are reviewing it. ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/06/nx-s1-5813497/iran-war-strait-hormuz-updates) | [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/5/7/explosions-heard-in-iran-as-state-media-reports-clashes-with-us-navy) | [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/06/world/live-news/iran-war-trump-strait-of-hormuz))

2. **Ukraine: Russia's Victory Day Ceasefire Begins Midnight Friday** — Russia's declared 48-hour ceasefire for Victory Day (May 8-9) took effect at midnight local time Friday with no air raid alerts reported across most of Ukraine in the opening hours. The ceasefire comes after a week of competing declarations: Ukraine had announced its own separate May 5-6 ceasefire, which Russia did not acknowledge; Russia threatened "massive missile strikes on Kyiv" if Ukraine violated the May 8-9 truce. Russian forces attacked Ukrainian cities with drones and ballistic weapons on May 7, the day before the ceasefire, and fighting continued throughout the competing ceasefire windows. Whether either side holds through Victory Day remains to be seen. ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/05/g-s1-120129/russia-declares-a-truce-in-ukraine-to-mark-victory-day) | [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/05/04/russia-unilaterally-declares-victory-day-ceasefire-while-zelenskyy-tables-own-truce))

3. **Hungary: Péter Magyar Takes Office Saturday, Ending Orbán's 16-Year Rule** — Péter Magyar is set to be sworn in as Hungary's prime minister on Saturday, following his landslide April 12 election victory (53.6% of the vote, 138 of 199 parliament seats — a supermajority). Magyar is a 45-year-old lawyer and former Orbán ally who turned whistleblower in 2024; his Tisza Party is pro-EU and anti-Russia, a 180-degree shift from the Fidesz government. With a supermajority, he can amend Hungary's constitution and has said he'll limit future PMs to two terms and root out corruption. This is the biggest political realignment in Central Europe since Poland's 2023 election. ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/04/13/nx-s1-5783657/peter-magyar-hungary-prime-minister-orban-election) | [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/13/who-is-peter-magyar-hungarys-new-leader-who-trounced-viktor-orban))

4. **US Tariffs at One Year: Moody's Says "Significant Damage"** — One year on from Liberation Day, Moody's Analytics chief economist Mark Zandi called the data "definitive" — the tariffs have caused significant economic damage, with job growth essentially stalled except in non-traded sectors like healthcare. Fed analysis estimates the tariff regime has cost roughly 19,000 additional monthly jobs; the average household is paying about $1,500/year more. After the Supreme Court's February ruling limiting IEEPA tariff authority, the Trump administration shifted to Section 122 of the Trade Act (a flat 10-15% on all imports) as the enforcement mechanism. Supply chain layoffs are accelerating — 32% of supply chain managers reported layoffs in May, up from 16% in April. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/06/liberation-day-trump-tariffs-damage-economy-moody-zandi/) | [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/economy/tariffs-may-have-cost-us-economy-thousands-jobs-monthly-fed-analysis-reveals))
