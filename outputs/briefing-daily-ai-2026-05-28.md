# Morning Briefing — 2026-05-28

## AI Tools, Tech & Advancements

1. **Kilo Code — Roo's community fork is surging** — Roo Code was archived on May 15, and its community-maintained fork, Kilo Code, has taken off — going viral in particular on Chinese developer platforms (Bilibili) as a Cursor alternative with active, fast-moving contributors. The open-source momentum is real: no VC, no marketing, just devs shipping. Worth watching if you care about the Cursor ecosystem. ([GitHub](https://github.com/Zijian-Ni/awesome-ai-agents-2026))

2. **Google Antigravity CLI lands for terminal-first devs** — Announced at Google I/O on May 19, Antigravity CLI is a lightweight terminal companion to Google's Antigravity 2.0 agentic dev platform. You spin up and interact with Google agent harnesses without ever leaving the shell — natural target for the Claude Code / Cursor crowd who live in terminals. It's being discussed alongside Gemini 3.5 Flash's strong agentic benchmark performance as evidence Google is serious about the developer stack this cycle. ([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/all-the-news-from-the-google-io-2026-developer-keynote/))

3. **Gemini Spark: Google's personal agent does the work, doesn't just answer** — Also out of I/O, Gemini Spark takes actions on your behalf — booking, organizing Gmail and Calendar, surface-level task completion. Pairing with it is "Daily Brief," a personalized morning digest drawn from your actual Gmail/Calendar. This is the clearest consumer-facing product from Google yet that treats Gemini as a doer, not a chatbot. Early user impressions are cautiously positive; the real test is how deeply it gets into workflows. ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/05/19/google-io-2026-news/))

4. **MCP crosses 97M monthly SDK downloads — quiet ubiquity milestone** — The Model Context Protocol hit 97 million monthly SDK downloads as of March 2026 with 10,000+ active public servers. Every major cloud vendor has adopted it natively. This isn't hype — it's the plumbing that now underlies most serious agentic apps. Developers building tools without MCP support are starting to feel the friction as the ecosystem locks in. ([AllInOneAICenter](https://allinoneaicenter.com/blog/new-ai-tools-may-2026))

5. **Claude Code at 87.6% SWE-bench, 1M context** — Anthropic's April Claude Code update (still the talk of agentic coding communities) bundles Opus 4.7 at 87.6% on SWE-bench Verified, a 1-million-token context window, and a new `xhigh` effort tier. UI screenshot resolution jumped from 1.15MP to 3.75MP, which matters a lot for browser-automation workflows. It's what's driving Anthropic's market share gains in enterprise dev tooling. ([Anthropic Agentic Coding Trends Report](https://resources.anthropic.com/hubfs/2026%20Agentic%20Coding%20Trends%20Report.pdf))

## AI Industry News & Shifts

1. **OpenAI launches DeployCo, a $4B AI consulting arm** — OpenAI's majority-owned DeployCo launched May 11, backed by $4B+ from TPG, Goldman Sachs, McKinsey, Bain Capital, and Capgemini among 14+ investors. This is a direct bet that the next trillion dollars isn't in model APIs — it's in implementation. It also puts OpenAI in direct competition with the consulting firms that are now its backers, which is a tension worth watching. Anthropic is running a similar ~$1.5B initiative. ([CIO](https://www.cio.com/article/4167787/openai-anthropic-expand-services-push-signaling-new-phase-in-enterprise-ai-race.html))

2. **KPMG rolls out Claude to all 276,000 employees globally** — KPMG's Digital Gateway Powered by Claude went live across 138 countries on May 19. This is one of the largest single enterprise AI deployments on record — and it's Anthropic, not OpenAI, that won it. Signals that Claude's positioning around safety and reliability is resonating with risk-averse professional services firms. Ramp's AI spend index already showed Anthropic overtaking OpenAI in US business payments last week; this compounds it. ([Hipther AI Dispatch](https://hipther.com/latest-news/2026/05/27/112423/ai-dispatch-daily-trends-and-innovations-may-27-2026-openai-anthropic-google-health-duckduckgo-rightsline-and-knowbe4/))

3. **Altman and Amodei walk back AI jobs doom — but Anthropic's own co-founder pushes back** — Both Sam Altman and Dario Amodei are now describing AI as a "productivity multiplier" rather than a job destroyer, softening months of doom rhetoric just as both companies pursue IPOs. But Anthropic co-founder Chris Olah, speaking at a Vatican AI ethics conference, directly contradicted Amodei: "There is a real possibility that AI will displace human labor at very large scale." The split inside Anthropic's own founding team — between the CEO selling enterprise and the researcher warning of mass displacement — is the most honest signal of where things actually are. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/27/ai-hype-doom-openai-anthropic))

4. **Google and Microsoft giving US government early frontier model access** — Google, Microsoft, and xAI have agreed to provide the Commerce Department's Center for AI Standards and Innovation with early access to frontier models for national security testing and risk evaluation before deployment. This formalizes the government-lab relationship established with OpenAI and Anthropic under prior federal AI safety initiatives. It's not regulation, but it's the closest thing to structured government oversight the US has right now. ([Claims Journal](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/05/05/337371.htm))

## World News

1. **US-Iran deal inching forward but not signed today** — Trump is meeting with his Cabinet today at a critical moment in negotiations. Iran has committed in principle to reopening the Strait of Hormuz and disposing of its stockpile of highly enriched uranium; the mechanics of how the stockpile is disposed of and the length of a uranium enrichment moratorium remain unresolved. Talks are mediated by Pakistan. Israel is concerned any deal that stops short of addressing Iran's nuclear program sets a dangerous precedent — Netanyahu spoke with Trump over the weekend. No agreement is expected today. ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/24/world/live-news/iran-war-news))

2. **Gaza stabilization force still a no-show, three months on** — The international force promised at Trump's January event to stabilize Gaza has not materialized. Separately, an Israeli strike near Tyre in southern Lebanon killed at least 14 people on May 27, according to Lebanon's health ministry. The gap between diplomatic announcements and on-the-ground reality in the region continues to widen. ([Reuters / US News](https://www.usnews.com/news/world))

3. **Europe baking: record May temperatures, wildfires** — The UK hit 35°C (95°F) at Kew Gardens on Tuesday, breaking its own record from Monday by more than a degree — both the hottest May days ever recorded in Britain. France is experiencing "unprecedented" heat for this time of year. Western Europe is running 10-15°C above normal this week. A wildfire broke out near Arthur's Seat in Edinburgh; hundreds of properties in southeast England lost water as demand spiked. Scientists are connecting this to the broader acceleration of European heat extremes. ([CNN Climate](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/26/climate/europe-heat-climate-intl))

4. **US economy: jobs holding, inflation climbing** — The US added 178,000 jobs in March; unemployment is at 4.3%. Wages continue to outpace inflation. But the OECD is warning inflation could reach 4.2% in 2026, driven in part by the Strait of Hormuz closure's supply-chain shock — which the CFR is calling a disruption potentially larger than Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine. The Iran deal timeline matters for the US economy, not just geopolitics. ([IMF](https://www.imf.org/en/blogs/articles/2026/04/14/war-darkens-global-economic-outlook-and-reshapes-policy-priorities) / [CFR](https://www.cfr.org/articles/the-u-s-economy-was-shaky-before-the-iran-war-now-its-in-real-trouble))

5. **Belgium: 4 dead after train hits school minibus at crossing** — A train struck a school minibus in Buggenhout, Belgium on May 26, killing at least four people including two children. Belgium's rail safety agency says footage shows crossing barriers were down and the red light was active when the crash happened, raising questions about driver behavior or barrier failure. Investigation is ongoing. ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/))
