# Weekly Opportunity Scan — 2026-06-08

## Top 5

1. **Loyalty Devaluation Alert Engine** — Proactive program-change alerts before your points lose value; no warning when Hyatt jumped 67%, Capital One/Emirates dropped 25%, and Aeroplan expanded pricing — all in May, all in the same month. Desperate user: miles/points hoarders watching programs quietly enshittify while they sleep. Distribution: r/churning, r/awardtravel, frequentmiler.com readers, Wayloft newsletter. ([FrequentMiler — Hyatt Devaluation](https://frequentmiler.com/mayday-hyatt-to-launch-a-brutal-new-world-of-hyatt-chart-in-may-2026/))

2. **GEO Copilot for Local Service Contractors** — Auto-generate FAQ schema pages, optimize Google Business Profile service descriptions, and monitor AI citations for trade-specific queries; desperate user: plumber/HVAC/electrician who is invisible in 78% of AI local-intent searches while ChatGPT recommends competitors with better-structured pages. Distribution: contractor Facebook groups, PHCC/ACCA trade associations, Jobber/Housecall Pro partner integrations, Klariqo's launch on PH this week validates the buying segment. ([ContractorMarketingPros — GEO Guide](https://contractormarketingpros.net/blog/why-geo-for-contractors-win-more-jobs-from-ai-search/))

3. **Non-Engineering Offer Decoder** — Standalone tool that decodes new-grad offer letters: RSU vesting cliff mechanics, total comp vs. base, benefits dollar value, market benchmark comparison, negotiation script; desperate user: spring 2026 UMich/Big-10 grads holding offers right now who don't understand their equity package. Distribution: campus career portals, r/cscareerquestions, LinkedIn early-career. ([Teal](https://help.tealhq.com/en/articles/10305790-offer-analysis-tool) is closest competitor but buried deep inside a large job-search suite — not standalone)

4. **Vibe-to-Production Guided Remediation** — Not a checklist — a step-by-step action plan: submit GitHub repo, get a plain-English diagnosis of what's broken, plus the exact Cursor/Claude Code prompt to fix each issue; desperate user: solo founder who vibe-coded an MVP, knows it's fragile (43% of AI code changes fail in production), but has no senior engineer to help harden it before launch. Distribution: r/SideProject, r/vibecoding (89K members), Lovable/bolt.new user communities. ([Microsoft Rayfin](https://thenewstack.io/microsoft-build-2026-rayfin-replit-vibe-coding/) fills the enterprise/Fabric end — the indie solo layer is still unowned)

5. **AI License Rationalization for Small Teams** — SaaS that tracks which AI subscriptions a team actually uses vs. pays for, surfaces context-switching costs, and recommends a minimal stack; desperate user: startup ops lead watching 52% of AI licenses go unused while 88% of the team reports productivity damage from constant tool-switching. Distribution: r/startups, HN, ProductHunt, Slack/Discord operator communities. ([AI fatigue stats 2026](https://shibumi.com/blog/ai-fatigue-statistics-2026/))

## Killed This Week

- **Agentic PM Workspace (generic)** — killed because ChatPRD (100K users), Scriptonia, BuildBetter, Figma AI PRD Generator, and Productboard Spark all compete for the same PRD-writing tier; only surviving angle (spec → deployed prototype) requires engineering depth that's too narrow to build standalone while at Okta.
- **EU AI Act Compliance Checker** — killed because August 2026 transparency rules are a one-time deadline, not recurring revenue; legal liability risk for a solo founder; Vanta/OneTrust will own this tier before the deadline hits.
- **Solo Founder AI Agent Ops Health Check** — killed because requires API log ingestion infrastructure, open-source observability tools (Langfuse, AgentOps, Arize) are free substitutes, and maintenance burden is 3× what a solo founder with a full-time job can absorb.
- **Community Engine** — week 9, no signal for 9 consecutive scans; permanently off the list.
- **Second Brain (horizontal)** — killed because Obsidian is at 1.5M users with 22% YoY growth, and Buildin.ai, AFFiNE, Capacities, and Taskade Genesis are all competing. The horizontal PKM market is crowded from every direction.
- **Award Calendar Heatmap** — killed for good; AwardHack now covers this lane and no differentiated angle survives.

## Watch List

- **"Will the Points Game Survive?" newsletter angle** — FrequentMiler's May 22 podcast episode asked this question out loud; the anxiety is real. A focused devaluation-alert email sequence is the natural top-of-funnel for opportunity #1 and costs almost nothing to start. Not a standalone product — a distribution wedge for the devaluation alerter.
- **AI Code Quality Audit → service-to-product path** — 43% of AI code changes require production debugging (VentureBeat, June 2026). A $500–$2,000 audit engagement for vibe-coded MVPs could fund productization. High-touch, not Okta-compatible, but worth watching for someone building this into a productized service.
- **AI Fatigue Reduction Stack** — 88% of heavy AI users report burnout, 67% of devs spend MORE time debugging since adopting AI. Counter-positioning opportunity for tools that reduce AI surface area rather than expand it. Trend is building but no clear product gap yet — watch for 4 more weeks.

## Kill / Build Signal

**Wayloft — STRONG BUILD (week 9 carry, hottest signal yet):**
The Hyatt +67% devaluation (May 20), Capital One/Emirates 25% with zero warning (Jan 13), and Aeroplan pricing expansion (May 20) all hit in the same month. FrequentMiler framed the community question as "Will the Points Game Survive?" and Delta loyalists published "feel scammed" headlines on May 23. None of the existing tools — PointsYeah (deal/availability alerts), Seats.aero (flight availability), AwardWallet (expiration tracking) — monitor program-level devaluation events. The hook "your Hyatt points lost 20% of their value last month — were you warned?" is the best urgency framing Wayloft has had. Build the alerter.

**Second Brain — WEAK SIGNAL → deprioritize:**
Obsidian 1.5M users, multiple YC entrants in adjacent spaces. The only surviving angle is a vertical second brain (travel-hacker-specific) layered on existing tools. Not worth the build effort while Wayloft is unfinished.

**Community Engine — DEAD:**
9 consecutive weeks of no signal. Off the list permanently.
