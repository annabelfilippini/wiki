# Morning Briefing — 2026-04-29

## AI Tools, Tech & Advancements

1. **OpenClaw: The Most-Starred GitHub Project in History** — OpenClaw, an open-source personal AI agent that lets you run your own assistant via WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Slack, and 20+ other messaging platforms, just surpassed 347,000 GitHub stars — more than React, more than any project ever. Built by Austrian developer Peter Steinberger and launched in late 2025, it hit 250K in under four months. The April 25 release added a full TTS voice system with per-agent overrides, ElevenLabs v3 integration, and persona support. The buzz is about self-hosted, model-agnostic AI infrastructure you actually own — no API key handed to a third party, no subscription to manage. ([GitHub](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw), [OpenClaw Blog](https://openclaws.io/blog/openclaw-250k-stars-milestone))

2. **llamafile 0.9.2 + LocalScore (Mozilla)** — Mozilla's llamafile — which bundles an entire LLM into a single executable you just double-click, no Docker, no install — shipped v0.9.2 with LocalScore, a benchmark that measures how fast specific models run on *your* hardware and contributes results to a community-wide database. Runs on macOS, Windows, Linux, FreeBSD, OpenBSD, and NetBSD from one file. Getting real traction among developers tired of Ollama's setup complexity; the pitch is "AI that behaves like a normal program." Worth having in your toolkit for local, private inference on client projects. ([Mozilla AI](https://www.mozilla.ai/open-tools/llamafile), [GitHub](https://github.com/mozilla-ai/llamafile))

3. **Runway ML Gen-4 Still Owns AI Video — Months In** — No new release this week, but worth noting that Runway Gen-4 continues to dominate AI video discussions across r/AIVideo, creative communities, and YouTube tutorials months after launch. The staying power is the story: coherent camera motion and cinematic output that earlier generations couldn't approach, and no serious competitor has dethroned it. If video is part of any Wayloft content or marketing play, this is the current standard. ([Reddit r/AIVideo](https://reddit.com/r/AIVideo))

## AI Industry News & Shifts

1. **OpenAI Missed Q1 Targets — and Chip Stocks Felt It** — The Wall Street Journal reported yesterday that OpenAI fell short of its Q1 revenue goals and its internal 1 billion weekly active user target. CFO Sarah Friar privately warned colleagues that aggressive capex commitments — tied to sprawling data center deals — could outpace revenue if growth doesn't accelerate. Oracle and CoreWeave shares dropped on the news, reflecting how deeply AI infrastructure bets are tied to OpenAI's trajectory. The narrative shift matters: for a year the question was "can anyone catch OpenAI?" — now it's "can OpenAI sustain its lead while Anthropic and Google Gemini close in?" ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/28/openai-reportedly-missed-revenue-targets-shares-of-oracle-and-these-chip-stocks-are-falling.html), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/28/openai-cfo-sam-altman-missed-revenue-target/))

2. **Google Gives DoD Unrestricted AI Access; Anthropic Is Now a "Supply-Chain Risk"** — After Anthropic refused to grant the Pentagon unrestricted use of Claude — drawing the line at autonomous weapons and domestic mass surveillance — the DoD branded Anthropic a "supply-chain risk," a designation normally reserved for foreign adversaries. Anthropic sued and obtained an injunction while the case proceeds. Google, meanwhile, quietly signed a deal granting the DoD use of its AI models on classified networks for "any lawful government purpose." Nine hundred and fifty Google employees have signed an open letter urging the company to reverse course. As of today, Axios reports Trump officials are drafting an executive order specifically designed to bring Anthropic back into the fold. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/28/google-expands-pentagons-access-to-its-ai-after-anthropics-refusal/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/04/29/trump-anthropic-pentagon-ai-executive-order-gov))

3. **The AI Market Is Quietly Becoming a Two-Horse Race** — Stack the signals from this week: Anthropic's annualized revenue crossed $30B with Google-backed compute behind it; OpenAI missed Q1 targets with a CFO warning on capex; Google has bet $40B on Anthropic plus committed 5 gigawatts of compute for five years; Amazon holds a separate $5B+ Anthropic deal. The "Big Three" era (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) is becoming a structure where Anthropic and Google are deeply intertwined and OpenAI navigates alone on Microsoft's infrastructure. How OpenAI responds in Q2 — whether through a model leap, aggressive pricing, or accelerated IPO — will determine whether this realignment is permanent. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-24/google-plans-to-invest-up-to-40-billion-in-anthropic), [WinBuzzer](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/04/29/openai-misses-targets-as-anthropic-and-google-gain-ground-xcxwbn/))

## World News

1. **Iran Ceasefire Stalls — Trump Unlikely to Accept Latest Offer (Day 60)** — Sixty days into the US-Iran conflict, the ceasefire declared April 7 remains fragile. Iran submitted a proposal: reopen the Strait of Hormuz, halt fighting, defer nuclear negotiations. Trump told advisers he's unlikely to accept it as written — his core leverage disappears if he reopens the strait without locking in nuclear terms first. The US naval blockade near Iranian ports continues; Brent crude is at $112/barrel; Iran has now also banned steel exports. The two sides are not far apart on some terms, per sources cited by CNN and PBS, but the gap on nuclear guarantees remains wide. ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-trump-strait-of-hormuz-iranian-offer-ceasefire-oil-gas-prices/), [PBS News](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/heres-what-to-know-about-ceasefire-negotiations-between-the-u-s-and-iran))

2. **UAE Quits OPEC After Nearly 60 Years** — The United Arab Emirates announced it is leaving OPEC and OPEC+, with the withdrawal formally taking effect May 1. The UAE has long chafed under Saudi-led production cuts while Iraq and Russia routinely exceeded their quotas; it wants room to reach its 5 million barrels-per-day capacity target by 2027 without cartel constraints. The Iran war — which disrupted the UAE's own export routes and rattled Gulf alliances — appears to have been the final push. US crude briefly crossed $100/barrel and Brent rose to nearly $113/barrel on the news. The longer-term signal: OPEC's cohesion is fracturing at the worst possible moment for the cartel. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/28/uae-leaves-opec-and-opec), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/28/oil-uae-opec-saudi-arabia.html), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/04/28/nx-s1-5802735/uae-leaves-opec-oil))

3. **James Comey Indicted Over "8647" Seashell Photo** — A federal grand jury in North Carolina indicted former FBI Director James Comey on Tuesday over a now-deleted Instagram post showing seashells arranged to read "86 47" on a beach — "86" being slang for "get rid of," and "47" widely read as a reference to President Trump. Charges include making a threat against the president and transmitting a threat in interstate commerce, each carrying a maximum of 10 years. Comey denied intent: "I didn't realize some folks associate those numbers with violence." It is the DOJ's second attempt to prosecute Comey under the Trump administration; the first case was dismissed last year. Critics call the prosecution a politically motivated stretch; supporters say threats against the president — however indirect — warrant legal action. ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/04/28/politics/justice-department-indicts-ex-fbi-director-james-comey-again), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/justice-department/james-comey-indicted-seashell-photo-officials-said-threatened-trump-rcna247022), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/04/28/nx-s1-5803167/james-comey-indictment))
