# Morning Briefing — 2026-05-23

## AI Tools, Tech & Advancements

1. **OpenAI Reasoning Model Cracks 80-Year Erdős Geometry Conjecture** — An internal OpenAI general-purpose reasoning model disproved the unit distance problem, a question Paul Erdős posed in 1946: how many point-pairs can sit exactly one unit apart on a flat plane. The model cracked it not with classical geometry tricks but by connecting the problem to algebraic number theory — a branch mathematicians hadn't applied here. It wasn't trained on math or scaffolded for proof search; it figured this out with general reasoning. Fields medalist Tim Gowers called it "a milestone in AI mathematics." Arul Shankar (leading number theorist): "current AI models are capable of having original ingenious ideas, and then carrying them out to fruition." ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/model-disproves-discrete-geometry-conjecture/), [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/ai-just-solved-an-80-year-old-erdos-problem-and-mathematicians-are-amazed/))

2. **Kilo Code v7 — Parallel AI Agents in VS Code, Free and Open Source** — Kilo Code v7 hit Product Hunt #1 Product of Day and Week (589 upvotes, 123 comments). The core feature: a parent agent that spins up multiple subagents, each handling a slice of a task concurrently — mimicking how an engineering team splits work. Also ships inline diff review (leave line-level comments on agent output like a PR review and feed them back as structured context) and multi-model comparisons. It's free and open source, which is the main differentiator vs. Cursor. Developers are calling it the open-source answer to Claude Code for multi-agent workflows. ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/kilocode), [Kilo.ai](https://kilo.ai/))

3. **Alteryx Agent Studio + MCP Server — No-Code Data Workflows → Autonomous Agents** — Debuted at Inspire 2026. Lets business analysts convert their existing Alteryx workflows into autonomous agents without touching code or going through IT. The MCP Server lets those agents connect to enterprise data sources directly. The pitch: all the Excel-era analysis your team already built can now run itself. Less buzzy than coding tools but pointed: if this pattern holds across enterprise software, the "analytics team" becomes an agent orchestration team. ([Crescendo AI](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates))

## AI Industry News & Shifts

1. **Trump Pulls AI Executive Order After Overnight Lobbying by Musk, Zuckerberg, and Sacks** — Hours before a planned White House signing on Thursday, the order collapsed. Elon Musk, Mark Zuckerberg, and AI/crypto czar David Sacks called Trump between Wednesday night and Thursday morning to kill it. The order would have required labs to share new frontier models with the government 90 days before public release — a voluntary framework, but still a framework. Trump's stated reason: "I think it gets in the way — we're leading China, we're leading everybody, and I didn't want to do anything to get in the way of that lead." The US AI governance posture is now definitively laissez-faire. Labs wanted no guardrails, and they got them. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/21/trump-ai-executive-order-postponed-why), [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-intelligence/ai-tech-brief/2026/05/22/ai-tech-brief-tech-ceos-blocked-executive-order/))

2. **OpenAI S-1 Confirmed Filed — $852B–$1T Target, Q4 2026 IPO** — OpenAI confidentially filed its S-1 with the SEC on May 22. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are leading. Key numbers: ~$2B/month in revenue, $25B annualized run rate as of March 2026, 50M consumer subscribers, 9M business users. Still unprofitable — losing $1.22 per dollar of revenue in Q1 2026. Confidential filing means the prospectus stays private until ~15 days before the roadshow. The race with Anthropic just added a public-market dimension: OpenAI goes for scale and liquidity; Anthropic is just now turning profitable. ([TECHi](https://www.techi.com/openai-ipo/), [Robo Rhythms](https://www.roborhythms.com/openai-ipo-filing-2026/))

3. **The Bigger Picture: AI Is Now Doing Original Research, Not Just Assisting It** — The Erdős solve isn't just a math story. It's the clearest data point yet that frontier reasoning models can generate genuinely novel ideas in expert domains — not retrieve, not interpolate, but originate. Combined with Karpathy joining Anthropic to use Claude to accelerate pre-training R&D (covered May 21), the pattern is: AI is now eating the research process that produces better AI. The feedback loop is closing. What changes when the bottleneck isn't human researcher hours but compute and iteration speed? That's the question labs are privately answering right now. ([Air Street Press](https://press.airstreet.com/p/state-of-ai-may-2026))

## World News

1. **Iran-US Nuclear Talks End Round 5 in Rome Without Breakthrough** — The fifth round of negotiations concluded May 23 in Rome. Omani mediator Badr al-Busaidi described "some but not conclusive progress." The core stall: the US is demanding full dismantlement of Iran's uranium enrichment capability; Iran's Supreme Leader Khamenei called that demand "excessive and outrageous." Iranian Foreign Minister Araghchi acknowledged the talks are "complicated" but said Oman's proposals created potential for progress. Both sides agreed to continue; no date for a sixth round announced. The framework under discussion would declare a ceasefire, open a 30-day negotiating window on enrichment, and lift sanctions in exchange for a moratorium — but the enrichment question is blocking everything. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/21/iran-us-deal-one-page-memo), [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/after-5th-round-of-nuclear-talks-iran-says-discussions-with-us-complicated/))

2. **Ukraine Gaining Ground — 29 Square Miles Net in a Week, Tactical Initiative Shifting** — In the week of May 12–19, Ukraine gained a net 29 square miles of territory (up from 12 sq miles the prior week). The Economist's 30-day estimate: Ukraine has recovered 204 sq km. There were 253 combat engagements in the past 24 hours, with the heaviest fighting in Pokrovsk (52 assaults repelled). Ukraine also struck Starobilsk in Luhansk Oblast overnight. ISW assessment: Ukraine is regaining tactical initiative across multiple sectors. Russia launched 124 drones toward Ukraine overnight on May 22. Total Russian personnel losses since Feb 2022: approximately 1.35 million, with 880 killed or wounded in the past 24 hours. ([Kyiv Post / ISW](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/76700), [Russia Matters](https://www.russiamatters.org/news/russia-ukraine-war-report-card/russia-ukraine-war-report-card-may-20-2026))

3. **US Households Paying More Despite Tax Cuts — Tariffs Erase Most of the Benefit** — The One Big Beautiful Bill's tax cuts are being largely offset by Trump's tariffs, which economists estimate cost the average US household $1,500–$1,700 per year. The net effect of combined OBBBA + tariffs is a tax increase for every income group except the top 5%. The Tax Foundation calculates tariffs offset roughly one-third of the OBBBA's long-run economic benefit while paying for less than half its cost. Earlier tariff refunds ($166B flowing) and Walmart price signals offered a counter-narrative, but the household-level math is unfavorable for most Americans. G7 finance ministers were in Paris this week discussing the global economic outlook against this backdrop. ([Tax Foundation](https://taxfoundation.org/blog/big-beautiful-bill-impact-deficit-economy/), [Spokesman-Review](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2026/apr/22/big-beautiful-bill-boosts-tax-refunds-but-tariffs-/))
