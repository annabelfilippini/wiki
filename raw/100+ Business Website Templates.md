Launch a professional business website fast with these templates. They’re built to communicate what you do and why it matters, with clear service pages, pricing, testimonials, FAQs, and strong calls-to-action for inquiries or bookings. You’ll also find options for essentials like contact/location blocks, hours, menus, portfolios, and basic ecommerce—so they work for trainers, gyms, real estate, startups, bakeries, food trucks, and more.

This is an aggregate collection from Squarespace, Shopify, Wix, WordPress, Webflow, Duda, and Framer. While some templates aren’t small-business-specific, they’re easy to tailor with your branding, copy, and images. All are fully responsive for a smooth experience on phone, tablet, and desktop.
