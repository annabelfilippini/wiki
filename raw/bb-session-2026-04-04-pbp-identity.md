## Context

PBP needed clarity on three questions: what business is this (data product vs media vs hybrid), what's the lead product, and how to approach the redesign. Three separate decisions made in sequence.

## Key Decisions

- **Business identity:** Data-powered media company — not pure data product, not pure media. Content SEO drives 100K visitors; data layer (Portal Score, price tracking, 428 paddles) monetizes them. Dropping content velocity would decay traffic in 6 months. Both engines matter; data is the differentiator but media is the distribution.
- **Lead product:** Consumer data product (price alerts, premium comparisons) over brand-tournament marketplace or Pikolai-as-product. Already has the infrastructure. Tournament marketplace is Phase 2 — requires B2B sales motion. Pikolai is brand, not product.
- **Redesign approach:** Ship, Learn, Redesign phased (Approach C) over big-bang rebrand or surgical-fix-only. Phase 1: fix affiliate + price alert CTAs (days). Phase 2: targeted redesign informed by Clarity + email data (weeks). Phase 3: full rebrand when metrics justify. Solo-founder math — each phase de-risks the next.

## New Frameworks / Patterns

- **Validate Before Redesigning** pattern confirmed — phased approach preferred over big-bang, same as Worth-It before extension on Wayloft.

## Killed / Deferred

- **Full Data-Powered Rebrand (Approach B)** — deferred, not killed. Right direction but premature without data from Phase 1.
- **Pure data product identity** — rejected. Can't drop content engine that drives all the traffic.
