# Advice: Share at least two pieces of advice with incoming UMSI students…

 **Advice:** Share at least two pieces of advice with incoming UMSI students about being successful at an internship. Consider including:
* The skills that were most important to your success at this internship 
* Advice for how to make the most of an internship experience
* Other pieces of advice you have 
* **Note: Please include some context for why you are sharing each piece of advice** (e.g. What could someone who takes your advice gain from doing this? What is the purpose of your proposed actions?)


 **Impact:** (1) Describe the overall impact this internship has had on your future career goal(s). (2) Name a next step you will take toward your goal(s). (3) Describe the reasoning behind the goal(s)/next step(s) based on your experience in your internship. In your response, you could reflect on: 
* What feedback did your supervisor share with you, and how might that shape your plans for continued professional development? What coursework or experiences could you participate in during the academic year that would be beneficial to your job search and future career? Provide specific examples of the next steps you plan to take for your career and professional development.
* Was your internship compatible or incompatible with your personal values? Did this impact your overall satisfaction with your experience? Consider what your critical factors are for job satisfaction. How might you seek out job opportunities that are in alignment with your values or critical factors for satisfaction?
