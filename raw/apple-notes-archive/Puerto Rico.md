We tried WWOOFing for spring break 😳 Would not recommend… 

I thought it would be a good idea to drag my 3 sweet friends to a random farm in Puerto Rico for 5 days to work on a rare fruit, coffee and chocolate farm. Sounds wholesome, I know. I really had no idea what we had signed up for. 

When they say 6 hours a day working on the farm they mean it. One day we spent 6 hours cutting down trees with a chain saw, dragging them up mountain sides, chipping the trees into mulch and then distributing the mulch wherever our host, Jeff, wanted it placed. The next day we spent our time weeding around coffee trees in the middle of the woods, gabby got 9 bee stings, I got ATTACKED by bugs and lydia left w a major sun burn 🤣, repeat that a few times and we threw in the towel and left the farm early. Sorry Jeff 😬

Advice - don’t wwoof unless you are desiring some serious manual labor and definitely don’t do it over your senior spring break (my bad ☺️) 

Anyways, we are spending our last few days surfing it up in aguadilla and arebico! No complaints, we have a good story now!!!
