# Waterski Email Draft


EMAIL 1 (to be send to summer bridge)

Hello, 

My name is Annabel Filippini and I'm reaching out to introduce you to an exciting opportunity that you may not be aware of yet - the University of Michigan Waterski Team. 

With the warmer weather upon us, there's no better time to dive into the exhilarating world of waterskiing right here on campus. Whether you're a seasoned water sports enthusiast or looking to try something new, our team offers a welcoming community where you can develop your skills, make new friends, and enjoy the warm summer days on the water. In this email I will provide you with some essential information including how to get involved and some upcoming events. 

PRACTICING

We practice with our lovely boat on Belleville Lake, where we have a course to practice slalom and trick skiing. We normally send out 2-3 boats a day in the given time frames: 6-12 PM, 12-4 PM, and, 4-8 PM. Schedules usually come out over the weekends for the upcoming week. Normally, we carpool to our boat and each boat will cost around $5 for boat gas and $2 for the car driver per person. 

**SKIING HAS ALREADY STARTED! YOU CAN SIGN UP** <u>**[HERE ](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1xKSBmyWBscPefXYmBqzj1YWmiySgdMW1EXp-GzpNAgc/edit#gid=0)**</u>
 
**How to fill out your availability**:
1. Write your name under skier (unless you are a boat driver - certified by our team)
2. Green marks = yes/available and red marks = no/not available
3. Once complete, check the box next to your name (if it’s not checked, you must not want to ski)
4. Availability is usually due on Friday or Saturday, and the schedule for the next week will be sent out on Sunday

SLACK and GOOGLE DRIVE

Join the Slack group for the team through this[ ](https://michiganwaterskiteam.slack.com/join/shared_invite/zt-gv8zhaw6-V3QFukNOV8I7WPWjzQB~4g#/)[link](https://michiganwaterskiteam.slack.com/join/shared_invite/zt-gv8zhaw6-V3QFukNOV8I7WPWjzQB~4g#/)! Slack is the main way that we communicate. It is an app on Apple and Android. We also have a shared Google Drive where we share everything from resources on how to ski, a guide to collegiate skiing, the O-Board contacts and much more helpful info. If you want to join, email me at [annabelf@umich.edu](mailto:annabelf@umich.edu). 

DUES 

Dues this summer are $175. But you can play set by set while you try out the team to see if it is something you want to become more involved in! You pay your dues by venmoing @M_waterski.  These dues help us maintain our lovely boat. If you don’t have Venmo, or have any financial concerns, you are more than welcome to contact our treasurer, Katya who will help you out. Her phone number is **(646) 477-0833.** 

Summary: 
1. Fill out your availability 
2. Email me to be added to the Slack and Google Drive 
3. Pay your dues
4. Attend Diag Day on May 6th from 1-4 to meet us, play games and learn more about the team 

Lastly, I want to mention that we are having a waterski camp May 11-12th. Here is the link with more information. [https://events.umich.edu/event/121553](https://events.umich.edu/event/121553)  See you there! 

We would absolutely love to have any and all skiers this summer. Your skill level does not matter! If you have any questions I am more than happy to answer them! 

Ski ya soon :)

Best, 
Annabel Filippini
