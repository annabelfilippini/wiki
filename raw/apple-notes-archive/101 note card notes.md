# 101 note card notes

- What is the purpose of this type of presentation 
The purpose of a special occasion speech is to stir the audience’s emotions to make them feel a certain way. You are aiming to celebrate, commemorate and inspire the individual or individuals you are speaking about. 

- What does it entail/ what key points should you make 
This speech is more personable and it is important that you adapt to the occasion of the speech. It should involve an attention grabbing statement and the thesis or main take away should be stated at the beginning of the speech with stories or memories to follow. Typically there are praises and appreciations to follow as well as well wishes or future aspirations to end the speech. 

Special occasion speeches are typically brief and should be practices thoroughly
