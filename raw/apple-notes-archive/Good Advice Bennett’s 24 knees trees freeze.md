# Good Advice Bennett’s 24 knees trees freeze

Slalom
-  Don’t snap around the ball, start your turn early
- Never stop moving, you should never be flat
- Finish your turn by pointing your front knee across the wake
-  Roll shoulders back and chest up

Trick
-  More gradual setting of tricks, don’t yank then tank the hit

Jump
-  For people cutting - eyes to the top left corner of the ramp
-  Skis shoulder width apart
-  Hold position on ramp into the landing 
-  Turn shoulders away from the boat

General
- for new skiers on jump skis have them pinch the rope in between the skis while sitting in the water for stability
