# T-shape model

- Horizontal bar has categories that you need to be fluent in to become future you (the change maker) 
- Vertical bar should have the category you’d like to specialize in - where you will achieve mastery 
- it might give people gage an understanding of what skills they have and what they need to acquire but there is not really an explanation of how to acquire these skills. 
- We think setting goals would be more helpful - you could draw it out to make it a visual thing and have like stairs to reach your overall goal at the top of the stairs.
