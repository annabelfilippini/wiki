# Football presentation


All touches - Any time where the running back would have the football in their hand. Hand off from quarterback. As a reminder before we play the video - notice that the overlay is not touching any part of the quarter back and shows complete body of player without leaving too much space. 


Receptions - Anytime running back is throwing the ball and they catch it. 

Targets - Anytime the ball is thrown to them whether they catch it or not
