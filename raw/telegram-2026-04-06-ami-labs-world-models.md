# Yann LeCun's AMI Labs Raises $1.03B to Build World Models

**Author:** Anna Heim
**Published:** March 10, 2026
**Source:** TechCrunch

## Key Details

- **AMI Labs** secured $1.03 billion in funding to develop world models — AI systems designed to understand and predict physical environments.
- **Yann LeCun** serves as Executive Chairman of AMI Labs.
- **Alexandre LeBrun** is CEO of AMI Labs.

## Notable Quote

> "My prediction is that 'world models' will be the next buzzword. In six months, every company will call itself a world model to raise funding."
> — Alexandre LeBrun, CEO of AMI Labs

## Technical Focus

The company aims to develop world models — a category of AI technology focused on creating systems that can learn and simulate how the physical world operates, enabling better prediction and decision-making capabilities.

## Context

The article positions this funding round within broader AI development trends, with LeBrun's comment suggesting upcoming market saturation as other companies adopt similar positioning for fundraising purposes.
