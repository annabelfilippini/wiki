## Idea

Take a photo of someone's outfit (e.g., street style) and say "this is a cute outfit, where are the clothes from?" — an AI agent would:
1. Identify each clothing item in the photo (tops, bottoms, bags, shoes)
2. Find where to buy each item (or closest matches)
3. Return shopping links
