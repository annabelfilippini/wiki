## Brand Voice

### Personality
| Trait | What it means | What it doesn't mean |
|-------|--------------|---------------------|
| **Authoritative** | We know this sport deeply. Data-backed opinions. Confident recommendations. | Not preachy, not academic, not "well actually..." |
| **Independent** | We call it like we see it. No brand tells us what to write. | Not contrarian for the sake of it |
| **Passionate** | We love pickleball. It comes through naturally. | Not hype-y, not sycophantic, not "🔥🔥🔥" |
| **Pioneering** | We were first, and we keep innovating. AI, real-time data, new tools. | Not Silicon Valley jargon, not "disruptive" |
| **Accessible** | A 3.0 rec player and a 5.0 pro both feel at home here. | Not dumbed down, not gatekeeping |
| **Transparent** | We show our data sources. We disclose affiliates. We timestamp everything. | Not apologetic about how we make money |

### Voice Do's and Don'ts

**DO:**
- Lead with insight, not introductions ("The Selkirk Power Air dominates the 16mm category" not "In this article we'll review...")
- Use data as proof ("Rated 92/100 across 847 player reviews")
- Acknowledge both sides ("Incredible power, but the learning curve is real")
- Write like you'd talk to a friend who plays — knowledgeable but not stiff
- Use "we" for Pickleball Portal, "you" for the reader
- Reference our history when it adds credibility ("We've tracked this brand since 2018")

**DON'T:**
- Use emojis in UI or content (Lucide icons only)
- Start with "Great question!" or any sycophantic opener
- Say "disrupting" or "game-changing" (overused)
- Use filler phrases ("It's worth noting that...", "In today's world...")
- Fabricate data, stats, testimonials, or social proof — ever
- Apologize for affiliate links (frame as: "we test it, we link it, we earn a small commission — it's how we stay independent")

### Tone Spectrum

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Paddle reviews | Confident expert | "This is the best control paddle under $150. Period." |
| News coverage | Sharp, current | "USA Pickleball just dropped new kitchen rules — and the community is split." |
| Tournament content | Excited, practical | "Registration opens tomorrow for PPA Houston. Here's what you need to know." |
| Deals | Urgent, honest | "Selkirk Power Air dropped 22% — lowest price we've tracked in 6 months." |
| Brand/B2B | Professional, data-driven | "Our audience of 50,000+ monthly visitors skews 35-55, high-income, gear-obsessed." |
| About/Origin | Warm, authentic | "We started Pickleball Portal in 2017 because nobody was covering this sport seriously." |

## Visual Identity

### Logo

**Concept:** A hexagonal badge containing a stylized pickleball paddle in motion — the paddle strikes a ball, with swoosh lines conveying energy and action. The hexagon functions as a "window" or portal into the sport.

**Logo Variants:**
1. **Horizontal lockup** — Icon + "PICKLEBALL PORTAL" text (for navbar, headers)
2. **Stacked lockup** — Icon above text (for social, print, brand materials)
3. **Icon only** — Hexagonal badge (for favicon, app icon, avatars)
4. **Monochrome white** — All-white on colored backgrounds
5. **Monochrome dark** — All-dark on light backgrounds

**Logo Usage Rules:**
- Minimum clear space: 1x height of the hexagon on all sides
- Minimum size: 32px for icon, 120px wide for horizontal lockup
- Never stretch, rotate, or recolor outside the approved palette
- On dark backgrounds: use white text variant
- On light backgrounds: use dark green text variant
- On brand green backgrounds: use all-white monochrome

### Color Palette

| Color | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|-------|-----|-----|-------|
| **Portal Green** (Primary) | `#22c55e` | 34, 197, 94 | CTAs, links, accents, active states, badges |
| **Dark Green** | `#104E29` | 16, 78, 41 | Logo fill, premium dark elements |
| **Charcoal** | `#1a1a2e` | 26, 26, 46 | Text, dark backgrounds, navbar |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Backgrounds, text on dark |
| **Light Gray** | `#F9FAFB` | 249, 250, 251 | Section backgrounds, cards |
| **Muted Green** | `#22c55e` @ 10% | — | Affiliate button backgrounds, highlight areas |
| **Orange (Accent)** | `#F97316` | 249, 115, 22 | Trending/hot indicators, urgent badges |
| **Red (Warning)** | `#EF4444` | 239, 68, 68 | Cons markers, stale data, errors |

**Color Rules:**
- Green is dominant — it should be the first accent color a visitor notices
- Dark backgrounds for hero sections, navbar, and "premium" feeling areas
- White/light gray for content areas (readability first)
- Never use green text on green background
- Affiliate buttons: green text on 10% green background (not solid green)

### Typography

| Role | Font | Weight | Usage |
|------|------|--------|-------|
| Headlines | System sans-serif (Inter/Geist) | 700-900 | Page titles, section headers |
| Body | System sans-serif | 400 | Article text, descriptions |
| UI / Buttons | System sans-serif | 500-600 | Navigation, CTAs, labels |
| Monospace | System mono | 400 | Data, scores, timestamps |

**Note:** The logo uses a custom athletic condensed italic typeface for "PICKLEBALL" — this is logo-only, not used in web content.

### Iconography
- **Lucide React** is the ONLY icon library
- No emojis anywhere in the UI or content
- `Bot` icon marks AI-powered features
- `ShieldCheck` / `ShieldX` / `ShieldQuestion` for approval badges
- Icons are 16-24px in UI, monochrome, matching text color or Portal Green

### Photography
- **Real photos only** — no AI-generated images for heroes, products, or lifestyle shots
- Hero images: real tournament courts, aerial shots, action photography
- Product images: clean product shots from manufacturer or Cloudinary
- Contributor photos: headshots with green ring border
- When no photo available: use CSS gradient placeholders (charcoal → dark green), never broken image icons

## "Window" Metaphor — Usage Guide

The "window" concept is central to our brand. Use it naturally, not forced.

**Good usage:**
- "Your window into the world of pickleball"
- "A window into paddle performance" (for Portal Score)
- "Your personal window" (for My Portal / personalized feed)
- "Opening a window into the business of pickleball" (for brands portal)

**Don't overuse it.** Once per page max. It's a brand concept, not a verbal tic.

## Content Categories & Voice

### For Players (Consumer)
- Gear reviews, strategy guides, tournament info, deals
- Tone: knowledgeable friend who plays at your level (or slightly above)
- Always: recommendations with reasoning, never just "it's good"

### For Businesses (Brands Portal)
- Market data, sponsorship opportunities, industry analysis
- Tone: professional peer, data-forward, ROI-minded
- Always: verified metrics, no aspirational numbers

### For the Community
- News, memes, controversy, culture
- Tone: passionate insider, opinionated, engaging
- Always: multiple perspectives, never one-sided hot takes

## Affiliate & Monetization Voice

### Disclosure (Standard)
> **Independent & Unbiased** — Pickleball Portal earns a small commission when you purchase through our links. This never influences our ratings or recommendations. It's how we stay independent and keep the lights on.

### Positioning
- We are NOT a store — we're an authority that happens to link to stores
- We test and rate FIRST, monetize SECOND
- Price comparison is a SERVICE to users, not a sales funnel
- "We help you find the best price" not "Buy now through our link"
